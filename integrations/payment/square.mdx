Square is a payment processing solution for both online and in-person transactions, including POS terminals.

<Info>
  All processors are configured in one place: **Settings → Integrations → Payment Gateway →
  Configure**, which opens the **Payment Gateways** page.
</Info>

---

## Connect it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Payment Gateways page">
    **Settings → Integrations → Payment Gateway → Configure**, then find the **Square** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Payment fee">
    Type **0** if you do not surcharge for card payments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Integrate">
    Continue with the login flow on Square's side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set it as your default">
    Tick **Set as default card payment option** if Square is the processor charges should run
    through.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Access token**, **Refresh token** and their expiry dates fill in automatically and are
read-only.

---

## Good to know

**Square reports card funding** (credit, debit or prepaid), so it can honour a credit-cards-only
processing fee.

Square payment links stay up until they are paid.

---

## The payment fee

The **Payment fee (%)** is a percentage added on top of the amount you charge a customer:

- Leave it empty or **0** → **no fee is added**. Charging $200 bills the customer exactly **$200.00**.
- Enter **3** → a **3% fee** is added. Charging $200 bills the customer **$206.00** ($200 + $6 fee).

Only the fee of the processor you set as your **default card payment option** is applied — it does
not depend on which card the customer has. The customer sees the breakdown on the charge screen and
on the receipt emailed to them once the payment goes through.

<Tip>
  Tick **Set as default card payment option** on the processor you actually want charges to run
  through. That checkbox, not the order of the cards, is what decides.
</Tip>

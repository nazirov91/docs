title: "Zelle and manual payments"
description: "Record payments the customer sends themselves — Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, Cash App and anything else."
---

Zelle is a U.S. instant bank-to-bank transfer service. In BeRocker it is a **record-only** method:
the customer sends the money themselves and an agent records it with a receipt attached. No card is
ever charged.

<Info>
  Payment processors are configured in **Settings → Integrations → Payment Gateway → Configure**,
  which opens the **Payment Gateways** page. See
  [Payment gateways](/settings/integrations) for the full picture.
</Info>

---

## What BeRocker asks for

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Zelle Email** | The address customers send to. |
| **Zelle Number** | The phone number customers send to. |

These appear on the payment instructions the customer receives.

---

## Set the default and the fee

The other record-only methods need no configuration at all. When recording a payment an agent can
choose **Zelle**, **Venmo**, **PayPal**, **Cash App** or **Other** — the catch-all for a wire,
check, cash or a regional app.

<Warning>
  A recorded payment is a bookkeeping entry, not a charge. Confirm the money actually arrived before
  you record it, and attach the receipt.
</Warning>

The **Payment fee (%)** on the Payment Gateways page is added on top of what you charge:

- Empty or **0** → no fee. Charging $200 bills the customer exactly **$200.00**.
- **3** → a 3% fee. Charging $200 bills the customer **$206.00** ($200 + $6 fee).

Only the fee of the processor set as your **default card payment option** is applied — it does not
depend on which card the customer has. The customer sees the breakdown on the charge screen and on
the emailed receipt.

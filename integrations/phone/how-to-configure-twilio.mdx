Twilio is a cloud platform for SMS, voice and WhatsApp. It is the most common choice for BeRocker
because one account covers texting, calling and WhatsApp.

---

## What you need from Twilio

Have these three values open before you start, from your
[Twilio Console](https://console.twilio.com):

| Value | Where it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Account SID** | The Twilio Console dashboard. |
| **Auth Token** | Next to the Account SID — reveal it to copy. |
| **Message Service ID** | Messaging → Services. Create a Messaging Service if you do not have one. |

<Warning>
  Your Auth Token is a password for your whole Twilio account. Paste it into BeRocker and nowhere
  else.
</Warning>

---

## Connect it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    **Settings → Integrations → Twilio → Configure**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the three values">
    Account SID, Auth Token and Message Service ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Status to Active and save">
    The card should show **Status: Active** in green.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point your number at it">
    **Settings → Profile → User Contacts** → add your Twilio number with **Provider = Twilio**, once
    with **Channel = Call** and once with **Channel = SMS**, and tick **Is Default?**.

    → [Profile](/settings/profile)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you send at volume

Twilio requires **10DLC registration** for application-to-person SMS to US numbers. Unregistered
traffic gets filtered by the carriers, not by Twilio — so messages simply stop arriving with no
obvious error.

Register your brand and campaign in the Twilio Console before you turn on follow-up sequences, and
keep an eye on [Messaging health](/settings/messaging-health) afterwards.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Status stays **Not connected** | The Account SID or Auth Token has a typo, or the token was rotated in Twilio. |
| Messages send but never arrive | 10DLC registration, and the failure rate on [Messaging health](/settings/messaging-health). |
| Cannot select Twilio when adding a contact | The integration is not Active yet. |
| Calls fail but texts work | You added the number for SMS only. Add it again with Channel = Call. |

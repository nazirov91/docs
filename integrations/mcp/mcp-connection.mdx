BeRocker offers an **MCP (Model Context Protocol) connection** so you can talk to your CRM from
[Claude](https://claude.ai), ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor or another AI tool. Once connected, you
can ask plain-English questions about your leads, analytics, pricing and load board posts, and the
answers come straight from your BeRocker account.

<Note>
  Everything is scoped to your company, and each person sees only what their role allows on screen.
</Note>

## What is MCP?

MCP is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to outside tools and data. The BeRocker MCP
connection gives your AI tool a read-first window into your CRM.

- **No exports, no spreadsheets.** Ask a question and get a live answer.
- **Your role decides what it sees.** Each person signs in with their own CRM account.
- **Changes are always confirmed.** Anything that would change something, such as posting a load,
  first shows exactly what will change and only goes ahead after you confirm.

## Turn on MCP and choose who can use it

Access is managed in **Settings → Team and security → MCP**. The tab is shown only to people whose
role may manage MCP access (administrators by default). Everyone else can still connect if their
role allows it; they just cannot change these settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on MCP for the company">
    At the top, the **MCP** switch (*Lets your team connect Claude, ChatGPT and other AI tools.*)
    turns the connection on or off for everyone. Off means nobody can connect, administrators
    included, and the tab says *MCP is off for everyone. The settings below are kept for when you
    turn it back on.*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide whether MCP may change things">
    Two more company switches start off:

    - **Allow changes**: lets roles with the matching permission change lead sources and lead
      distribution through MCP, each change confirmed first. Turning it on asks
      **Allow changes through MCP?**
    - **Full CRM access**: lets roles with the matching permission do what they can do on screen
      through MCP, each change confirmed first. Turning it on asks **Turn on full CRM access?**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set what each role can do">
    **What each role can do through MCP** is a table with one row per role and one switch per
    permission (hover a column name to see what it allows). Every role can connect and read what it
    sees on screen. Without the first permission in the table, a role cannot use MCP at all.
    Administrators are marked **Always on**. A column that needs a company switch that is off shows
    *Company switch off*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the people">
    **People** lists everyone with their role, an **MCP** switch and status (**Allowed**,
    **No access**, **Blocked** or **Off for the company**), and whether they are **Connected**.
    Switch a person off to block them and end their connection; blocking overrides their role.
    **End connection** disconnects someone now (and revokes their access keys); they can connect
    again unless blocked. People with no role cannot connect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Access keys for scripts

Where your company has access keys, the MCP tab also has **Access keys** with **Create key**. A key
lets a script call MCP as one person and see what their login sees. Keys are read-only unless you
give them **Powers**, which must expire within 90 days and apply at most a set number of
**Changes per hour**. Only an administrator can create a key that acts as someone else. The key is
shown once (*Copy this key now. It won't be shown again.*) and is sent as
`Authorization: Bearer <key>`. Every key a person holds and their own chat share 600 calls a minute.
Use **Revoke** to stop a key; this cannot be undone.

## Connect your AI tool

Copy the connection address from **Your MCP address** on the MCP tab (it ends in `/mcp`), then open
**How to connect** for ready-to-copy steps. Connections made on the older address keep working.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Claude (web, desktop or phone)">
    In Claude, go to **Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector**. Name it `BeRocker` and paste
    the address. Click **Connect**, sign in with your CRM email and password, and allow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="ChatGPT">
    **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced** and turn on **Developer mode**. Then **Create**, name
    it `BeRocker`, paste the address and choose OAuth authentication. Sign in with your CRM email and
    password, and allow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claude Code, Cursor and other MCP clients">
    The MCP tab gives you the exact command for Claude Code and the settings block for Cursor and
    other clients. Add it, then sign in when asked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only add connectors from developers you trust. The BeRocker connector is provided and maintained
  by the BeRocker team.
</Note>

To check it works, ask *"What time is it for me in the CRM?"*, then *"Show my 5 newest leads."*

## Things to ask

- _"Show me a summary of all leads this month"_
- _"What are the conversion rates by lead source for Q1?"_
- _"Find all new leads from California that don't have offers yet"_
- _"Give me the details for lead #4521"_
- _"Show me the chat history for lead 892"_
- _"How often do customers ask about enclosed transport?"_
- _"Export every conversation from last month as JSONL"_
- _"Compare company performance this month vs last month"_
- _"What's the estimated price for shipping a sedan from LA to NYC?"_
- _"Check if lead 350 has any active posts on SuperDispatch"_
- _"Prepare a post for lead 350 on CentralDispatch and show me what will be sent"_

## Some of the tools it uses

The tools your AI tool is offered depend on what your role has been given on the MCP tab. A tool
that is missing is not broken; an administrator can grant it there. These are some of the most used.

### Analytics and reporting

| Tool | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `get-dashboard-summary` | Lead counts by status, conversion rate, response times, lead costs and top sources. |
| `get-lead-sources-report` | Lead performance by source: counts, conversion rates, costs and offer stats. |
| `get-company-performance` | Company-wide KPIs, revenue, costs and response times, with period-over-period comparison. |

### Leads

| Tool | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `search-leads` | Search leads by status, dates, location, broker, customer name, source and offer status. |
| `get-lead-details` | Full details for one lead by ID or lead number. |
| `get-lead-communications` | Chat messages and call transcripts for one lead. |

### Conversations in bulk

| Tool | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `get-conversation-stats` | How much history matches a set of filters before you export it. |
| `search-conversations` | Find individual messages and call transcripts across every lead, newest first. |
| `export-conversations` | Every lead's conversations in one call, inline or as a JSON, JSONL or CSV file. |

<Tip>
  Ask for `get-conversation-stats` before `export-conversations` on any large range, so the AI tool
  knows how big the job is.
</Tip>

### Load boards

| Tool | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `get-lead-posts` | Existing load board posts for a lead. |
| `prepare-loadboard-post` | A read-only preview of what would be posted to SuperDispatch or CentralDispatch. |
| `execute-loadboard-action` | Post, repost or delete listings, or create or cancel a dispatch, after you confirm the preview. Needs your role's load board permission. |

### Pricing

| Tool | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `get-pricing-comparison` | Compare BeRocker AI, SuperDispatch and CentralDispatch prices, by lead or by route. |

## Good to know

- **Your data is private.** Everything is scoped to your company, and a person limited to their own
  shipments sees only those.
- **Date filters use `YYYY-MM-DD`.** State filters use abbreviations (`CA`, `NY`, `TX`).
- **Changes are always confirmed.** The AI tool shows a preview first and needs your yes.
- **Rate limit:** 600 calls a minute per person.

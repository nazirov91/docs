## Super Dispatch API Access

To get access to the **Super Dispatch API**, first ensure API access is included in your subscription, then request credentials from their support team.

### How to Obtain API Access

<Steps>
  <Step title='Verify Subscription'>
    The **Shipper API** is normally available for *$250/month*. When used through BeRocker, this fee
    is waived — giving you full API access at **no additional cost**.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Request Credentials'>
    Contact Super Dispatch technical support to receive your `ClientID` and `ClientSecret`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Contact Super Dispatch Support

- Email: `support@superdispatch.com`
- Phone: `+1 (816) 974-7002`
- In-App: Use the chat icon in the bottom-right corner of the Carrier TMS or Shipper TMS dashboard.

### Add Credentials in BeRocker

<Steps>
  <Step title='Open Super Dispatch Integration Settings'>
    In BeRocker, go to `Settings -> Integrations -> Super Dispatch`.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Enter API Credentials'>
    Map the fields as follows:
    - **Username**: Enter your `ClientID`
    - **Password**: Enter your `ClientSecret`
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Pricing Insights (Optional)

`X-API-KEY` is used for Super Dispatch Pricing Insights service.

If you have this subscription (or want to request it), contact Super Dispatch using the same support channels above, then enter your key in the `X-API-KEY` field.

## What you get once it is connected

| Capability | Where you see it |
| --- | --- |
| Post loads without leaving the CRM | The **Carrier** tab of a shipment |
| Carrier offers come back into the CRM | The same tab, and the **SD Requests** queue on [Dispatch](/using/dispatch) |
| **Live driver GPS** and pickup / delivery **ETA** | The [Dispatch](/using/dispatch) map |
| Pickup and delivery statuses | They move the shipment automatically to **Picked Up** and **Delivered** |
| Recommended price as your pricing engine | [Pricing](/settings/pricing) |

## Then configure the behaviour

Posting behaviour is separate from the connection. In
[Carriers and Loadboards](/settings/carriers-and-loadboards) you decide:

- Whether to **unpost from the other loadboard** once a carrier is secured (recommended on).
- Whether to **auto-import Super Dispatch orders** worked outside the CRM (off by default — it can
  add a lot of orders).
- Whether **Super Dispatch price updates** are applied to the CRM (off by default; either way the
  change is recorded on the lead's timeline).

Orders that could not be imported are listed under **Super Dispatch sync** with a retry.

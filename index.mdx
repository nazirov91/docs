## The short version

<Steps>
  <Step title="Leads arrive">
    From your website, a lead provider, an email your inbox parses, or a shipment an agent adds by
    hand. Every lead lands in **Inbox** and **Shipments** and gets assigned to an agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You quote and follow up">
    Send a quote from a template by SMS or email. Follow-up sequences chase the ones who go quiet,
    and **Follow up → Triage** tells you which conversations actually need a human today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The customer books">
    They sign the agreement and pay the deposit on your booking page. The lead becomes an order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You find a carrier">
    Post the load to Central Dispatch or Super Dispatch straight from the CRM, review offers,
    and dispatch the carrier you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The load moves and closes out">
    Pickup and delivery updates come back from the load board, the customer gets automatic status
    messages, and the money shows up in **Accounting**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  Not everything below is switched on for every account. What you can see depends on your **role**

---
title: "Step 4 — Adjust your templates"
description: "Read and rewrite every message that will go out in your name before you go live."
---

The default templates work, but they are not *your* voice. Read every one before a customer does.

---

## Where they are

**Settings → Templates**, with three tabs:

| Tab | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Events** | The moments that send a message — quote, order confirmation, dispatched, delivered, and so on. Each is tagged **Automatic** or **Manual**. |
| **Follow-up** | The timed ladder of messages that chases customers who go quiet. |
| **Health** | What your messaging is actually doing right now. |

Full page: [Templates](/settings/templates)

---

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with Quote">
    It is the message most customers will ever read from you. Get the wording right, on both **SMS**
    and **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write three or four versions of each">
    BeRocker picks one at random each time, so repeat customers do not get identical texts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the variables">
    Copy names exactly from the **Variables** tab. A misspelt variable renders as nothing and the
    customer gets a message with a hole in it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the follow-up sequence">
    First step inside the hour, then spread the rest out — something like 1 hour, 1 day, 3 days,
    7 days. Give it an end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Health tab">
    Quiet hours in the customer's local time, sensible pacing, and an opt-out footer on SMS.

    → [Messaging health](/settings/messaging-health)
  </Step>
</Steps>

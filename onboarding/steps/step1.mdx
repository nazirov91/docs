Before configuring anything, spend ten minutes learning where things are and how a lead moves
through the system. Everything after this will make more sense.

---

## The layout

<Card title="Tour the CRM" icon="compass" href="/get-started/tour">
  Every screen, what it is for, with pictures.
</Card>

The short version:

| Screen | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbox** | Talk to customers — SMS, email, WhatsApp and calls in one thread. |
| **Shipments** | See every lead and order as a table, grouped by status. |
| **Follow up** | Triage tells you which conversations need a human today. |
| **Dispatch** | Watch the loads that are already moving. |
| **Settings** | Configure everything. |

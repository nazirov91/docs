title: "Step 2 — Profile and phone numbers"

---

Every user does this for themselves. Until it is done you cannot message anyone.

---

## What to do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Profile">
    Check your name, display name and email under **User Info**. Your **display name** is what
    customers see on messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your contact channels">
    Under **User Contacts**, click **Add user contact** and add one entry per channel:

    - **Channel** — **Call/SMS (same number)**, Call, SMS, WhatsApp or Email
    - **Provider** — Twilio, RingCentral, Telnyx, Vonage, Quo, OpenPhone, Zoom Phone, SendGrid, Mailgun,
      Gmail
    - **Contact** — the number or address
    - **Is Default?** — tick it for your main line on that channel

    <Tip>
      A single number used for both **calls and texts** needs a Call contact *and* an SMS contact.
      Pick **Call/SMS (same number)** and it is saved as both in one go.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register RingCentral numbers">
    If your provider is RingCentral, click **Register** next to the number and complete the popup.
    Without it, calls and texts through RingCentral will fail.

    ![The Register button next to a RingCentral number](/images/steps/rc-register.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable two-step authentication">
    **Two Step authentication → Add authentication method**. Optional unless your company requires
    it — but recommended. **Login Sessions** below it lists where you are signed in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check it worked

Open any conversation in the [Inbox](/using/inbox) and look at the composer. If you see
**"No SMS / email sender available"**, a default contact is still missing.

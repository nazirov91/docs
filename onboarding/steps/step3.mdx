Admins only. This is where your team gets created and the incoming leads get shared out.

---

## Add each user

**Settings → Team and security → People → Add user** is a short wizard. The steps depend on the
role you pick on the first screen:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account — name, email, role and team">
    Email, name, role, team and the rest of the account details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phone and SMS — how they reach customers">
    For roles that talk to customers (a broker, for example): the numbers and email addresses this
    agent will work from. Required for a broker — they cannot work without a phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leads and pay — lead distribution and commission">
    For those same roles: whether they receive leads, how many per day, and their commission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay — optional salary and commission">
    For roles that do not talk to customers, this replaces the two steps above.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **People** tab then shows each person with whether they are **Ready to work** and whether they
**Receive leads**. The other tabs are **Teams**, **Two-step verification**, **Sign-in history** and
**User contacts**.

Full page: [Team and security](/settings/team-and-security)

---

## Then set the rota properly

**Settings → Lead Management → Lead distribution**:

- Quotas per agent per day — **0** means no leads that day.
- **Working hours** per day, or leave **Available 24 hours** on.
- **Can exceed limit** for the agents you trust with overflow.
- An **after-hours fallback** person, so a lead arriving at 2am is not left unassigned.
- Filter by **lead source** if different providers should go to different people.

Full page: [Lead distribution](/settings/lead-distribution)

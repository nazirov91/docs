**Settings → Profile** is your own account. Everything on this page affects only you, not the rest
of the team.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings/profile.png" alt="The Profile settings page with User Info, Two Step authentication, User Contacts and Login Sessions" />
</Frame>

The page has five blocks, in this order: **User Info**, **Two Step authentication**,
**Gmail connection**, **User Contacts** and **Login Sessions**.

---

## User Info

Your full name, display name, email, password and role.

**Display name** is what customers see when a message goes out under your name — use the name you
actually introduce yourself with, not your legal name.

Click **Edit Profile** to change any of it, including your password.

---

## Two Step authentication

Adds a second step to your sign-in.

- Click **Enable**, then confirm.
- Use **Add authentication method** to register how you receive the code.

<Tip>
  Strongly recommended for admins and anyone who can see customer payment data. Your company can
  also require it for everyone — see [Team and security](/settings/team-and-security).
</Tip>

---

## User Contacts — the numbers you work from

This is the block that makes messaging work at all. Each contact is one **channel** on one
**provider**.

The table lists each contact's **Channel**, **Provider**, **Contact**, **Default** (Yes / No),
**Status** and **Actions** (edit, delete).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add user contact">
    The **Add new user contact** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    - **Channel** — **Call/SMS (same number)**, **SMS**, **Call**, **WhatsApp** or **Email**.
      *Call/SMS (same number)* is a shortcut: enter one number once and it is saved as two
      contacts, one for calls and one for texts.
    - **Provider** — depends on the channel. Call or SMS: Twilio, RingCentral, Telnyx, Vonage,
      Quo or Zoom Phone. WhatsApp: Twilio. Email: Mailgun, SendGrid or Gmail.
    - **Contact** — the phone number (for Email the field is labelled **Email**). With Provider =
      Gmail the field becomes a **Gmail mailbox** picker listing the mailboxes connected in the
      Gmail connection block above.
    - **Status** — Active or Disabled.
    - **Is Default?** — tick it to make this your main line for that channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click **Apply** to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### The rules that trip people up

<Warning>
  **One entry covers one channel.** A number you use for both calls and texts needs a Call entry
  *and* an SMS entry — pick **Call/SMS (same number)** when you add it and both are created for
  you. Editing an existing contact only offers the single channels.
</Warning>

- You can have **one default per channel**: one default call number, one default SMS number, one
  default email.
- You can mix providers freely — RingCentral for calls, Twilio for SMS, SendGrid for email.
- Without a default SMS or email contact you will see
  **"No SMS / email sender available"** in the [Inbox](/using/inbox) and cannot message anyone.

### RingCentral needs registering

If the contact's provider is RingCentral, a **Register** button appears next to the number.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/steps/rc-register.png" alt="The Register button next to a RingCentral number in User Contacts" />
</Frame>

Click it, complete the RingCentral sign-in in the popup, and the number is authorised. Until you do,
calls and texts through RingCentral will fail.

## Gmail connection

> Connect your own Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox to send and receive chat emails as yourself.

This block only appears when the Gmail integration is enabled for your organization. Click
**Connect Gmail** and complete the Google sign-in. Once linked, the block shows the mailbox address
with a status tag — **Connected**, **Needs reauthorization**, **Error** or **Disconnected** — plus
**Test connection**, **Disconnect**, and **Reconnect Gmail** when the status is not Connected.

After connecting, add the mailbox as an **Email** user contact with **Provider = Gmail** (below) so
chat emails go out from it.

<Warning>
  On Google's consent screen, make sure **both** permissions are checked — **"Read your email
  messages"** and **"Send email on your behalf"**. Without both, the connection cannot complete and
  the page asks you to reconnect and approve both.
</Warning>

<Info>
  This is your personal mailbox for chat email. The mailbox that feeds
  [email parsing](/settings/email-parsing) is connected separately on that page.
</Info>

## Login Sessions

Every device signed in as you: **Country**, **Device**, **Last activity** and **IP**. Check it if
you suspect someone else has your password, and change your password immediately if anything looks
wrong. The block is only shown if your role can view login sessions.

Organization-wide session history for admins lives in
[Team and security → Sign-in history](/settings/team-and-security).

## Checklist

| Task | Required? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add a call number | Yes, to call | From any connected voice provider |
| Add an SMS number | Yes, to text | Pick **Call/SMS (same number)** if it is also your call line |
| Add an email | Yes, to email | From SendGrid, Mailgun or Gmail |
| Tick **Is Default?** | Yes | One per channel |
| Register RingCentral | If you use RingCentral | Otherwise calls and texts fail |
| Enable 2FA | Recommended | Required if your company enforces it |

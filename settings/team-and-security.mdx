**Settings → Team and security** is where you run your team.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings/team-and-security.png" alt="Team and security with the People, Teams, Two-step verification, Sign-in history and User contacts tabs" />
</Frame>

Five tabs: **People**, **Teams**, **Two-step verification**, **Sign-in history** and
**User contacts**. Each one is shown only to roles with the matching permission.

---

## People

One row per user with the columns **Member** (name, email and a *Deactivated* / *Blocked* chip
when they are not active), **Role**, **Team**, **Ready to work**, **Receives leads**, **Last
active**, **Added** and an **Edit** button.

**Ready to work** says what, if anything, stops the person from working, in the order you would
fix it: *Deactivated*, *Blocked*, *No phone number*, *No call number*, *No SMS number*, *Leads
off*, *Not signed in yet* or *Ready*. Hover it for the fix.

The **Receives leads** switch turns lead assignment on or off for that person without
deactivating their account — use it when someone is on holiday. It can only be switched on for
someone who already has a lead distribution rule.

**Search by name or email**, or filter by **Any status** and **Any role**. **Add user** opens the
creation flow.

## Adding a user

**Add user** opens the **Add a user** page. Nothing is saved until you press **Create user** on
the last step. The steps depend on the role: a **Broker** or **Dispatcher** gets three —
**Account**, **Phone and SMS**, **Leads and pay** — and every other role gets two — **Account**
and **Pay**. **Continue** moves forward, **Back** goes back, and a summary panel on the right
counts what is still missing before the person can sign in or take leads.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account">
    *Who they are and what they can do in BeRocker.*

    - **Full name**.
    - **Work email** — their login.
    - **Role** — Admin, Manager, Team lead, Broker or Dispatcher (see below).
    - **Team** — pick one.
    - **Display name** — the name customers see on messages.
    - **Password** — at least 8 characters (when editing: **New password**, blank keeps the
      current one).
    - **Require two-step verification when they sign in**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phone and SMS">
    Brokers and dispatchers only. *Brokers need a call and SMS number to work leads* — for a
    broker the numbers are required; for a dispatcher you can add them later.

    - **Calls** — **Provider** and **Phone number**.
    - **Texts (SMS)** — tick **Same number as calls**, or give a separate provider and number.
    - **Add WhatsApp or email** adds another channel row (**Channel** WhatsApp or Email,
      **Provider**, and the number or email).

    A RingCentral number still has to be **registered** by the agent from their own Profile page
    before it works.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Leads and pay (or Pay)">
    For brokers and dispatchers, **Lead distribution** first: the **Receives new leads** switch,
    then **Leads per day** and **Working hours timezone**. A user who already has a custom
    schedule is changed in [Lead distribution](/settings/lead-distribution) instead.

    Then **Set pay and commission** — optional, used for salary and commission reports:

    - **Fixed salary ($)** — leave it at 0 if they are commission-only.
    - **Shareholding fee (%)**.
    - **Commission tiers** — **From ($)** / **To ($)** / **Commission (%)** rows; **Add a tier**
      for each range of revenue.

    > For example: up to \$2,000 → 20%, \$2,001–\$3,000 → 22.5%, above \$3,000 → 25%.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create user">
    The last step's button reads **Create user** (or **Save changes** when editing). If the
    account was created but a later part failed, the page says so and offers **Save the rest**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then send the agent their login and ask them to open **Settings → Profile** and register their
number.

## Roles

| Role | As the app describes it |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Full access, including settings, billing and the team. |
| **Manager** | Oversees leads, dispatch and the team day to day. |
| **Team lead** | Leads a team of brokers and follows their work. |
| **Broker** | Works leads by phone, text and email. Needs a phone number. |
| **Dispatcher** | Moves booked loads to carriers. Needs a phone number. |

Roles carry a set of permissions, and permissions are what actually hide or show menu items and
buttons. If someone cannot see a screen, that is why. Roles are not edited in the app — ask
support if you need a different one.

## Teams

> Group people into teams to organize leads, reports and lead distribution.

Every user belongs to a team. **Create a team** opens a dialog with **Team name** and a
**Members** list (search users and tick them); finish with **Create team**. Each row shows the
team and its member count, and **Edit** reopens the same dialog (**Save changes**).

Teams are what the Dashboard's team filter and per-team reporting run on.

---

## Two-step verification

Under **Two-step authentication**, one switch: **Require two-step authentication for your team** —
*this will require any team member without two-step authentication to enable it the next time they
sign in.*

<Warning>
  Turning this on affects everyone who signs in afterwards. Tell your team before you flip it, or
  you will spend the morning on the phone.
</Warning>

---

## Sign-in history

Sign-in sessions across the whole organization — **Id**, **Member**, **Team**, **Role**,
**Device**, **Last activity** and **IP** — with **Search by name or email** and a **User Role**
filter. Check it after any suspicion of a shared or leaked password.

Individual users see their own sessions on their [Profile](/settings/profile) page.

---

## User contacts

Every contact channel for every user in one table (the same **Channel / Provider / Contact /
Default / Status** columns as the Profile page, filterable by **Contact**, **Channel**,
**Provider**, **Status** and **Default**), so you can see at a glance who has no default SMS
number and therefore cannot text anyone. You can edit or delete entries here too.

<Tip>
  This is the fastest way to diagnose "my agent's messages are not sending". Nine times out of ten
  there is no default contact for the channel.
</Tip>

**Settings → Team and security** is where you run your team.

The screen has seven tabs: **People**, **Teams**, **Roles**, **Two-step verification**,
**Sign-in history**, **MCP** and **User contacts**. Each tab is shown only to people whose role
includes the matching permission, so you may see fewer.

---

## People tab

One row per user with the columns **Member** (name, email and a *Deactivated* or *Blocked* chip
when they are not active), **Role**, **Team**, **Ready to work**, **Receives leads**, **Last
active** and **Added**. You can sort by **Member**, **Last active** and **Added**.

**Ready to work** says what, if anything, stops the person from working, in the order you would
fix it: *Deactivated*, *Blocked*, *No phone number*, *No call number*, *No SMS number*, *Leads
off*, *Not signed in yet* or *Ready*. Hover it for the fix.

Use **Search by name or email**, or filter by **Any status**, **Any role** and **Any team**.
**Clear filters** resets them. **Add user** opens the creation flow.

If any broker or dispatcher has no phone number, a yellow banner at the top counts them: *They
can't call or text customers until they do. Open them with Edit to add a number.*

The gear button at the end of each row opens a menu with **Edit** (opens the user's page) and
**Permissions** (see below). You only see the items your role allows.

---

## Turn lead assignment on or off for a person

The **Receives leads** switch in the People tab turns new-lead assignment on or off for that
person without deactivating their account. Use it when someone is on holiday.

- It shows **On** or **Off** next to the switch.
- It is greyed out for anyone who is not active, and for you if your role cannot edit users.
- It only works for someone who already has a lead distribution rule.

## Add a user

Click **Add user** in the People tab. The **Add a user** page opens. Nothing is saved until you
press **Create user** on the last step.

The steps depend on the role you pick. A **Broker** or **Dispatcher** gets three steps:
**Account**, **Phone and SMS** and **Leads and pay**. Every other role gets two: **Account** and
**Pay**. **Continue** moves forward, **Back** goes back, **Cancel** leaves without saving, and a
summary panel on the right counts what is still missing before the person can sign in or take
leads.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account">
    *Who they are and what they can do in BeRocker.*

    - **Full name**.
    - **Work email**: their login.
    - **Role**: any role from the Roles tab, including custom roles you created.
    - **Teams**: assign to one or more teams (optional).
    - **Display name**: the name customers see on messages.
    - **Password**: at least 8 characters. Share it with them securely; they can change it in
      their profile.
    - **Require two-step verification when they sign in**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phone and SMS">
    Brokers and dispatchers only. *Brokers need a call and SMS number to work leads.* For a broker
    the numbers are required; for a dispatcher you can add them later.

    - **Calls**: **Provider** and **Phone number**.
    - **Texts (SMS)**: tick **Same number as calls**, or give a separate provider and number.
    - **Add WhatsApp or email** adds another channel row (**Channel** WhatsApp or Email,
      **Provider**, and the number or email).

    A RingCentral number still has to be **registered** by the person from their own Profile page
    before it works.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Leads and pay (or Pay)">
    For brokers and dispatchers, **Lead distribution** comes first: *Choose whether new leads are
    shared with them.* Set the **Receives new leads** switch, then **Leads per day** and **Working
    hours timezone**. A user who already has a custom schedule is changed in
    [Lead distribution](/settings/lead-distribution) instead.

    Then switch on **Set pay and commission** if you want it. It is optional and used for salary
    and commission reports:

    - **Fixed salary ($)**: leave it at 0 if they are commission-only.
    - **Shareholding fee (%)**.
    - **Commission tiers**: **From ($)**, **To ($)** and **Commission (%)** rows; **Add a tier**
      for each range of revenue.

    Once pay has been saved for a user, it can be changed but not cleared.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create user">
    The last step's button reads **Create user**. If the account was created but a later part
    failed, the page says so and the button changes to **Save the rest**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Afterwards, send the person their login and ask them to open **Settings → Profile** and register
their number.

## Edit a user

In the People tab, click the gear button on the person's row and choose **Edit**. The **Edit
user** page has the same steps as adding a user. **Password** becomes **New password**; leave it
blank to keep the current one. Changes are saved when you press **Save changes**.

The **Permissions** button at the top right of the page opens the same Permissions dialog as the
People tab menu.

---

## Change one person's permissions

Use this to give or take away a permission for one person without changing their role.

1. Go to **Settings → Team and security → People**.
2. Click the gear button on the person's row and choose **Permissions** (or press **Permissions**
   on their Edit user page).
3. The dialog shows their **Role:** at the top. Pick a tab:
   - **Quick**: about ten plain-English switches. Each switch shows **On**, **Off** or **Partly**
     and saves the moment you click it. A message confirms *Switched on* or *Switched off* with an
     **Undo** button.
   - **Advanced**: every permission, grouped in sections with a "held of total" count. Search
     with **Search permissions, or a section name…** and use **Expand all** / **Collapse all**.
     Each permission shows where it comes from: **via role**, **added** (given to this person
     only) or **taken away** (removed for this person only). Switch permissions on or off, then
     press **Save changes**.
4. Optionally, **Start from a preset** (when presets are offered) sets the person's permissions to
   a ready-made set and moves you to the Advanced tab. Only this person changes, not their role.
   Nothing is saved until you press **Save changes**.

**Restore a taken-away permission:** on the Advanced tab, the **Taken away for this person** box
lists every permission removed from this person. Click **Restore** next to one to give it back,
then press **Save changes**.

Good to know:

- Only people whose role lets them manage people's permissions can change anything. Everyone
  else who can open the dialog sees the permissions read-only.
- For an Administrator, *Administrators hold every permission. Anything you switch off here is
  taken away from this person only.*
- While you have unsaved changes on the Advanced tab, the Quick switches are locked until you save
  or discard them.
- Closing with unsaved changes asks *Discard changes?*; choose **Discard** or **Keep editing**.
- If you change your own permissions, your access updates straight away.

## Create or edit a role

The **Roles** tab lists every role as a card with its name, a description, a **System** tag for
the built-in roles and the number of people who hold it. Use **Search roles…** to find one.

*System roles are built in and can't be deleted. The Administrator always keeps full access.
Changes apply in the app right away and never remove anyone's data or sign them out.*

The built-in roles are:

| Role | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Full access to everything: billing, integrations, teammates and roles. Always keeps every permission and can't be edited or deleted. |
| **Manager** | Runs day-to-day operations: manages leads, teammates, teams and carriers, and views reports. |
| **Team lead** | Leads a sales or dispatch team: follows and assigns the team's leads and tracks how the team is doing. |
| **Broker** | Sales agent: creates, quotes and works leads, calls and texts customers and takes payments. |
| **Dispatcher** | Handles the carrier side: posts and dispatches loads, manages carriers and drivers, and accepts offers. |

**To create a role:**

1. Click **New role**. The **New role** panel opens.
2. Enter a **Role name** (required) and an optional **Description**.
3. Choose the permissions, in any of three ways:
   - **Quick setup**: tick a bundle to switch on all of its permissions below; untick it to
     switch them off. A bundle that is only partly on shows **partly**.
   - **Start from a preset**: pick a ready-made set (for example the built-in roles' defaults:
     **Administrator**, **Manager**, **Team Lead**, **Broker**, **Dispatcher**, or **Blank (no
     permissions)**). This replaces the current selection, and nothing changes until you save.
   - Switch individual permissions on or off in the sections below. Each section has an **All**
     tick box and a count. Search with **Search permissions, or a section name…**. A tag shows
     how many are selected, for example *42 of 280 selected*.
4. Click **Create role**.

**To edit a role:** click **Edit** on its card, change the description or permissions, and click
**Save changes**. The name of a system role cannot be changed. The Administrator role cannot be
edited at all, so its button reads **View** and the panel says *The Administrator role always has
full access and can't be edited.*

Closing with unsaved changes asks *Discard changes?*.

Only people whose role allows adding, editing or deleting roles see **New role**, **Edit**,
**Duplicate** and **Delete**.

After you have a role, assign it to people on their Edit user page.

## Duplicate a role

On the **Roles** tab, click **Duplicate** on a role's card (*Create a copy with the same
permissions*). The **Duplicate** panel opens with the name *Copy of* plus the original name and
the same permissions. Change what you need and click **Create role**.

---

## Delete a role

1. On the **Roles** tab, click **Delete** on the role's card.
2. If people hold the role, the dialog warns how many and asks you to choose a role under **Move
   members to**. You must pick one before you can delete.
3. Click **Delete role**.

The role is permanently removed. Teammates keep their accounts and data; only their access level
changes. System roles cannot be deleted, so their **Delete** button is greyed out.

---

## Teams

> Group people into teams to organize leads, reports and lead distribution.

The **Teams** tab lists each team with its **Members** and when it was **Created**. Use **Search
teams or people** to find one.

- **Create a team** opens a dialog with **Team name** and a **Members** list (search users and
  tick them). Finish with **Create team**.
- **Edit** reopens the same dialog; finish with **Save changes**.
- The bin button deletes a team after you confirm (**Delete team**). The people on it keep their
  accounts, roles and leads; they just stop being grouped together. The app can refuse when
  something still relies on the team, for example lead distribution, and says why.

Teams are what the Dashboard's team filter and per-team reporting run on.

---

## Require two-step verification for everyone

On the **Two-step verification** tab, under **Two-step authentication**, one switch: **Require
two-step authentication for your team**. *This will require any team member without two-step
authentication to enable it the next time they sign in.*

The switch is greyed out unless your role allows changing it.

<Warning>
  Turning this on affects everyone who signs in afterwards. Tell your team before you switch it on.
</Warning>

## Sign-in history

The **Sign-in history** tab lists sign-in sessions across the whole company: **Id**, **Member**,
**Team**, **Role**, **Device**, **Last activity** and **IP**, with **Search by name or email** and
a **User Role** filter. Check it after any suspicion of a shared or leaked password.

Each person sees their own sessions on their [Profile](/settings/profile) page.

## Connect AI tools through MCP

The **MCP** tab lets your team connect Claude, ChatGPT or another AI tool to the CRM. Each person
signs in with their own account and sees only what their role allows. Only people whose role can
manage MCP access see this tab.

### Company switches

Three switches at the top apply to the whole company:

| Switch | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **MCP** | Lets your team connect Claude, ChatGPT and other AI tools. Off: nobody can connect, whatever their role says, Administrators included. |
| **Allow changes** | Change lead sources and distribution, each confirmed. Needed for the **Changes** column below. Off: MCP only reads (and posts loads where allowed). |
| **Full CRM access** | Do what the person can do on screen, each confirmed. Needed for the **Full CRM** column below. |

Turning on **Allow changes** or **Full CRM access** asks you to confirm first. Both are greyed out
while **MCP** is off. When MCP is off, a banner says *MCP is off for everyone. The settings below
are kept for when you turn it back on.*

### Your MCP address

**Your MCP address** shows the address to paste into the AI tool, with a **Copy** button. Click
**How to connect** for step-by-step setup for **Claude (web, desktop or phone)**, **ChatGPT**,
**Claude Code**, **Cursor and other MCP clients** and, when access keys are available,
**Scripts with an access key**. To check it works, ask *"What time is it for me in the CRM?"*,
then *"Show my 5 newest leads."*

### What each role can do through MCP

A table with one row per role and one switch per grant: **Connect**, **All shipments**, **Same
customer**, **Reports**, **Payments**, **Load boards**, **Changes** and **Full CRM**. Hover a
column heading for what it means. Without **Connect**, a role cannot use MCP at all. **Changes**
and **Full CRM** show *Company switch off* while their company switch is off. The Administrator
row is marked **Always on** and cannot be changed.

### Block a person or end their connection

The **People** list shows each person, their **Role**, an **MCP** switch with their status
(*Allowed*, *No access*, *Blocked* or *Off for the company*) and whether they are **Connected**.

- **Block a person:** switch their **MCP** switch off. Blocking overrides their role and ends any
  connection they have. Switch it back on to allow them again.
- **End connection:** click **End connection** on their row and confirm. Their MCP connection
  ends now, and any access keys they hold are revoked. They can connect again unless blocked.

People with no role cannot connect; a note counts them.

### Access keys

When access keys are available on your account, an **Access keys** section lists them: **Key**
(name, status and purpose), **Acts as**, **Reaches**, **Powers**, **Created**, **Last used** and
**Expires**. A key is read-only unless given powers, and every key a person holds shares their
600 calls a minute.

To create a key:

1. Click **Create key** (greyed out until MCP is on).
2. Fill in **Name**, an optional **Purpose** and **Acts as** (the person whose access the key
   uses). Only Administrators can make a key for someone else.
3. Under **What it can reach**, tick the features the key may read (all are ticked to start).
4. Optionally, Administrators can tick **Powers**. A power is only available if the chosen person
   holds it and the company switch is on. A key with powers must have **Changes per hour** set
   and must expire within 90 days. With **Full CRM access**, tick **What it can change**.
5. Set **Expires** (optional for a read-only key) and click **Create key**.
6. Copy the key from **Your new access key**. *Copy this key now. It won't be shown again.*

To stop a key, click **Revoke** on its row and confirm. Anything using it stops on its next call.
This cannot be undone.

## User contacts

The **User contacts** tab lists every contact channel for every user in one table (the same
**Channel / Provider / Contact / Default / Status** columns as the Profile page), filterable by
**Contact**, **Channel**, **Provider**, **Status** and **Default**. You can see at a glance who has
no default SMS number and therefore cannot text anyone. **Add user contact** adds one, and you can
edit or delete entries here too.

<Tip>
  This is the fastest way to diagnose "my agent's messages are not sending". Most of the time there
  is no default contact for the channel.
</Tip>

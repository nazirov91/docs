**Settings → Notifications** controls what BeRocker tells *you* about. It is per-user: your settings
do not affect anyone else on your team.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings/notifications.png" alt="The Notifications settings page with delivery details and the event list" />
</Frame>

<Info>
  Notifications only fire for **leads assigned to you**. No assigned leads, no notifications.
</Info>

---

## Step 1 — where they go

Two fields at the top: an **SMS** number and an **Email** address. Enter them and click **Save**.
There is a **test** action so you can confirm the destination actually receives something.

<Warning>
  Do **not** use the same number or email you already use as a customer-facing contact in
  [Profile → User Contacts](/settings/profile).

  If you do, your own notifications arrive as messages from an unrecognised number and pile up in
  [Interactions](/using/interactions).
</Warning>

<Info>
  SMS notifications are sent through **your own** phone number, so they count toward usage and
  charges on your VoIP provider account.
</Info>

---

## Step 2 — which events

Below that is the **Events** section, split into four categories. Tick the ones you want.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Leads">
    - **New Lead** — a lead was assigned to you.
    - **New Hot Lead** — a lead the source marked hot.
    - **Price Issue** — the pricing engine could not produce a price.
    - **Lead Assigned** — one of your leads was reassigned.
    - **Custom Vehicle Lead** — a lead with a vehicle that is not in the catalogue.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Chats">
    - **New Message** — a customer replied.
    - **User Agree** — a customer accepted the quote.
    - **User Opt Out** — a customer replied STOP.
    - **User Wants Call** — a customer asked to be called.
    - **Empty AI Response** / **AI Final Price Request** — the AI needs a human.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Payments">
    - **Payment Success**, **Payment Failed**, **Payment Refund**
    - **Payment Dispute**, and its **Won** / **Lost** outcomes
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Orders">
    - **New Order**
    - **New Offer**, **Offer Accepted**, **Offer Declined**, **Offer Cancelled**
    - **Load Board Order Cancelled**, **Contract Cancelled**
    - **Picked Up**, **Delivered**
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Changes apply as soon as you save.

---

## What to turn on

Start narrow and add rather than starting wide and drowning:

| If you are | Turn on |
| --- | --- |
| A broker working leads | New Lead, New Hot Lead, New Message, User Wants Call, User Agree |
| Handling money | Payment Failed, Payment Dispute, Payment Refund |
| Doing dispatch | New Offer, Offer Accepted, Picked Up, Delivered |

<Tip>
  If you find yourself ignoring notifications, that is the signal to turn some off — not to try
  harder. An alert you ignore is worse than no alert.
</Tip>

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing arrives | Confirm SMS/email works on your account at all, then re-check the saved number and address. |
| Notifications land in **Interactions** | Your notification contact details match your customer-facing ones. Change them. |
| Too many | Untick event types. Payments and Orders are usually the noisy ones for a broker. |
| Only some events arrive | Those events are unticked, or the lead is not assigned to you. |

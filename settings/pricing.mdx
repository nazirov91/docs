**Settings → Pricing** decides what a customer is quoted. Three tabs:
**Carrier pricing engine**, **Deposit price** and **Price tiers**.

## Carrier pricing engine

> Configure your preferred pricing engine for generating quotes. BeRocker AI is always available as
> the default option. To use Super Dispatch or Central Dispatch pricing, you'll need to provide your
> API credentials.

### Auto Price

> When a lead arrives, automatically calculate the price using the selected source and coefficient,
> and send the quote to the customer. Turn this off to quote incoming leads by hand.

The page tells you plainly which mode you are in:

- **Auto Price is ON** — new leads are priced and quoted automatically.
- **Auto Price is OFF** — *"New leads are not priced or quoted automatically — your team quotes them
  by hand."* The engine is still used for the price estimates shown when you add a vehicle or a
  shipment yourself.

### Choosing an engine

Under **Select auto price type** — shown whether or not Auto Price is on, because the engine also
drives the manual add-vehicle / add-shipment estimates:

| Engine | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **BeRocker AI Price** | Always available. The default. |
| **Super Dispatch Recommended Price** | Needs Super Dispatch connected, otherwise it shows **Not available**. |
| **Central Dispatch Price** | Needs Central Dispatch connected. |

Connect them in [Integrations](/settings/integrations).

### Correction factor

Under **Lead Auto Price**, the **Correction factor** (%):

> Percentage applied to the recommended price. 100% uses it as-is; lower reduces the quote, higher
> increases it.

At 100% nothing changes. Set anything else and the app spells out the effect with a worked example
(*"Auto-priced quotes will be 10% above the … — e.g. a … recommendation is quoted at …"*). Click
**Save** (shown only to roles that can set the auto price).

<Tip>
  Change the correction factor in small steps and watch your booking rate on the
  [Dashboard](/using/dashboard). It is the single fastest lever on both volume and margin, in both
  directions.
</Tip>

### Enclosed transport multiplier

Shown when Super Dispatch is configured. Super Dispatch returns one **open**-trailer price, and the
enclosed quote is derived by multiplying it by the **Enclosed multiplier** (between 0.1 and 10;
1.00 makes enclosed equal to open, 1.40 marks it up). Type a **Preview open price** and the
*Open price → derived enclosed quote* line shows the result before you commit with **Save
multiplier**.

This setting is saved independently of Auto Price.

<Info>
  The bottom of this tab also shows the **Load boards** cards, so you can connect Super Dispatch or
  Central Dispatch without leaving the page if their price is showing as **Not available**.
</Info>

## Deposit price

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings/deposit-price.png" alt="The Deposit price tab with lead-price brackets" />
</Frame>

The deposit is what the customer pays you at booking. It is set with **brackets** by lead price.

> This page controls the deposit a customer pays to book a lead. Each row is a price bracket. For any
> lead, the system picks the **first** bracket whose *Lead price from / to* range contains the lead's
> price, then works out the deposit from that bracket's settings.

The formula for each bracket is:

```text
deposit = (Percentage deposit × lead price) + Fixed deposit price
```

The table columns are **ID order · Lead price from · Lead price to · Fixed deposit price ·
Percentage deposit · Minimum deposit price · Actions**.

Each bracket has:

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead price from / to** | The price range this bracket covers. Leave **to** empty to mean "this price and up". |
| **Percentage deposit** | A percentage of the lead price. |
| **Fixed deposit price** | A flat amount added on top. |
| **Minimum deposit price** | The floor. If the calculated result is below it, the minimum is used instead. |

**Add new range** creates another bracket.

### Worked example

A bracket with 10% percentage, \$100 fixed and a \$250 minimum:

| Lead price | Calculation | Deposit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| \$2,000 | (10% × 2,000) + 100 | **\$300** |
| \$500 | (10% × 500) + 100 = \$150, below the \$250 minimum | **\$250** |

<Info>
  The final amount is rounded up to the nearest \$10.
</Info>

## Price tiers

> Offer your customers three price options instead of one — for example Standard, Rush and
> Expedited.

When enabled:

- The **booking page** shows all three options.
- The lead view gets a **tier switcher**.
- The **Update Price** form lets you edit all three tier prices independently.

### How the tiers get their numbers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tier 1 is calculated">
    A new lead's Tier 1 gets the automatically calculated price — auto price + correction factor +
    deposit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tiers 2 and 3 are seeded from it">
    Using the **price markup** percentages you set here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="After that every tier is independent">
    You can edit each tier's price and deposit on the lead without affecting the others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The customer picks one">
    On the booking page. The lead's price is always the selected tier's price, and that is the
    price on the contract and the payment. A tier left at $0 is not shown to the customer and
    cannot be selected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each tier card has a **Name** (e.g. *Standard*), **Subtext** (e.g. *Refundable until pickup*),
**Description** (shown to the customer on the booking page), **Price markup** (%) and **Deposit
increase** — as a **%** over the Tier 1 deposit or a fixed **$** amount per vehicle. On Tier 1 the
markup and deposit controls are locked: it always starts from the auto-calculated price and keeps
the deposit from the deposit price table. The ocean fee is never marked up. A live preview shows
how the three tiers will look on the booking page, with each tier's deposit listed under the
cards; click **Save**. Only users who can change pricing settings see **Save**.

<Warning>
  New leads start on Tier 1. Markup changes apply to leads priced from now on and to leads whose
  tiers were never set. A lead whose tiers are set (it was priced with tiers on, or a tier was
  chosen or edited) keeps them until it is re-priced or you use **Recalculate Tiers 2–3 from
  Tier 1**. Every tier needs a name before you can save.
</Warning>

### Turning tiers on or off

Switching the **Price tiers** toggle asks you to confirm, then saves. It never changes the price of
any lead, so leads that are being booked, signed or paid are not affected.

- **Turning tiers off:** every lead keeps its current price as its single price — including a tier
  the customer already chose. The booking page shows one price again.
- **Turning tiers on:** every lead's current price becomes its Tier 1, and Tiers 2 and 3 are
  calculated from it with your markups. A lead that was on a tier before keeps that tier if its
  price has not changed since. A customer who still has the old page open keeps the lead's
  current price when they continue.

### Tiers on the lead

- **Price tier** in the lead view shows the selected tier. Click it to move the lead to another
  tier; a tier at $0 cannot be selected.
- **Update Price** lets you edit each tier's price and deposit. The tier marked **Selected** is the
  lead's price. **Recalculate Tiers 2–3 from Tier 1** rebuilds Tiers 2 and 3 from Tier 1 with the
  current markups; the lead stays on its selected tier.
- **Add new vehicle** on a lead that is on Tier 2 or 3: enter the Tier 1 (base) price — that tier's
  markup is added automatically.
- **Apply prices to lead** in the pricing engine sets a new Tier 1 and recalculates Tiers 2 and 3;
  the lead stays on its selected tier.

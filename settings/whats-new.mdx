**Settings → What's New** is the CRM's change log: every update in plain words, newest first.
It tells you what is new, what got better, what was fixed, and what works differently now.

Everyone on your team can open it. It does not depend on your role.

---

## Open What's New

You can reach it two ways:

- **Settings → Updates → What's New**.
- The help menu: **What's New** (*Latest changes to the CRM*).

The top of the page shows the date of the most recent update (**Last update:**).

---

## Read an update

Updates are grouped by month, newest first, ten to a page. Each update shows:

- A type tag: **New** (something you could not do before), **Improved** (something that already
  existed now works better), **Fixed** (something that was not working right now does) or **Heads
  up** (something now works differently and is worth knowing about). Hover the tag for its meaning.
- The date.
- A title and a short summary, sometimes with a list of highlights.
- **What you need to do:** when the update asks something of you, for example a setting to turn on.
- The areas of the CRM it touches, such as *Shipments & Quotes*, *Phones & Calls* or *Settings &
  Team*. Click an area to show only updates for that area.

When there are more than ten updates, the bottom of the page says which ones you are looking at
(for example *1–10 of 85 updates*) and lets you move between pages.

---

## See what changed since your last visit

Updates that arrived since you last opened the page are marked **New since your last visit**, and a
note at the top says how many there are, for example *3 updates since your last visit*. If you
have filters on, click **Show them** to clear the filters and see them at the top.

The marks stay in place while the page is open. The next time you open it, those updates are no
longer marked.

Good to know:

- The first time you open the page, nothing is marked new.
- What you have seen is remembered in your browser, so a different browser or computer, or a
  private window, starts afresh.

---

## Filter or search updates

- **Type:** pick **All**, **New**, **Improved**, **Fixed** or **Heads up**. Each option shows how
  many updates it has.
- **Area:** pick an area from **All areas**, or click an area on any update. Clear it with the
  cross.
- **Search updates:** type a word to search titles, summaries, highlights and actions.

If nothing matches, the page says *No updates match these filters.* Click **Show all updates** to
clear the filters.

The filters are part of the page address, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.

---

## If the page does not load

The page says *What's New could not be loaded. Check your connection and try again.* Click
**Retry**.

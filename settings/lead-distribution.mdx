**Settings → Lead Management → Lead distribution** is the rota for incoming leads. Every lead that
arrives is assigned to an agent according to what you set here.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings/lead-distribution.png" alt="The Lead distribution table with a row per agent and a column per day" />
</Frame>

---

## The table

One row per **Team / Broker**, one column per day:

**Mon · Tue · Wed · Thu · Fri · Sat · Sun · Timezone · Can exceed limit · Total leads · Status**

Each day cell is that agent's **leads per day** quota.

<Info>
  A quota of **0** means that agent gets no leads that day. That is how you exclude someone without
  deactivating them.
</Info>

---

## Working hours per day

Click a day cell and you get a small panel with:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Available 24 hours** | On by default. Leads can arrive for this agent at any hour. |
| **Start / End** | Turn the switch off to set a shift window instead. |
| **Apply M-F** | Copy this cell to Monday–Friday. |
| **Apply to all** | Copy this cell to every day of the week. |

Under each cell you will see either `24h` or the window you set, so the rota is readable at a
glance.

**Timezone** is per agent — someone working from another state gets leads during *their* hours.
Leave it blank to use the organization default.

---

## Can exceed limit

Ticked, an agent can be given leads beyond their daily quota when volume spikes and nobody else is
available. Unticked, the quota is a hard ceiling.

<Tip>
  Give it to your strongest closers and leave it off for new agents. Nothing burns a good lead like
  giving it to someone with fifteen already open.
</Tip>

---

## After-hours fallback

> Leads that arrive when no agent is on shift are assigned to this person.

Pick a user, or leave it as **No fallback (hold)** and leads wait until someone comes on shift.

<Warning>
  If you set shift windows but no fallback, a lead arriving at 2am sits unassigned until morning. If
  you sell on speed of response, name a fallback.
</Warning>

---

## Filtering by lead source

If you buy leads from several providers and want them to go to different people:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the source">
    Above the table is a row of tags starting with **LP Not specified** — the general rota used
    for every source without its own. Click **Add specified lead provider**, pick the source in
    the **Add custom distribution for** dialog and **Apply**. It appears as a new tag.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set quotas for that source only">
    Click the tag to switch the table to that source. Set 0 for the agents who should never get
    that provider's leads, and a real quota for the ones who should.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat per source">
    Each source keeps its own rota. The **×** on a tag removes that source's custom rota and it
    falls back to **LP Not specified**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This is how you keep expensive provider leads on your best agents while your website leads spread
across the team.

---

## Status

The per-row status switch turns distribution on or off for that agent without touching their
quotas — useful for holidays.

The **Receives leads** switch on the People list in
[Team and security](/settings/team-and-security) does the same thing from the other screen.

---

## Setting it up for a new agent

For brokers and dispatchers, the **Leads and pay** step of the **Add user** flow has a
**Receives new leads** switch with **Leads per day** and **Working hours timezone**, so a new agent
gets a rota at the moment you create them. See [Team and security](/settings/team-and-security).

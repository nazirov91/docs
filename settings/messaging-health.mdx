**Settings → Templates → Health** answers the question every other messaging screen dodges:
*what is actually happening right now?*

Every guard around sending — the per-number pace, the quiet window, the number check — makes a burst
impossible. None of them tells you whether a customer is waiting two minutes or two hours. This tab
does.

## Sending numbers

One row per number you send from:

| Column | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Attempts** | Messages sent from this number. |
| **Delivered** | Confirmed delivered. |
| **In flight** | Sent, no outcome yet. |
| **Failed** | Confirmed failures. |
| **Failure rate** | Failures as a share of **confirmed outcomes** — not of everything attempted. |
| **Confirmed coverage** | How much of this number's traffic reached a definitive outcome at all. |
| **Needs attention** | Flagged as unhealthy. |

<Info>
  Read the failure rate *next to* the confirmed coverage. Most texts never get a carrier receipt, so
  a failure rate calculated over everything attempted would be a receipt-coverage score wearing a
  health score's clothes. That is why both numbers are shown.
</Info>

<Warning>
  A number marked **needs attention** is on its way to being blocked by carriers. Rest it, check
  what you have been sending from it, and make sure your opt-out footer is on.
</Warning>

## The queue

| Figure | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | Messages scheduled but not sent. |
| **Due now** | Ready to go this minute. |
| **Due within the hour** | Coming up. |
| **Waiting for morning** | Held by quiet hours until the window opens — tomorrow's wave, visible today. |
| **Next due at** | When the next message goes out. |
| **Minutes to drain** | How long the overdue backlog takes to clear at the current pace. |

<Tip>
  A big **waiting for morning** number is normal, not a fault. It is the overnight follow-ups queued
  up for the release window.
</Tip>

---

## Opt-outs

How many customers replied STOP in the last 7 and 30 days. A climbing opt-out rate is the earliest
warning that your messaging is too aggressive — well before a carrier blocks a number.

## The settings

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **SMS per number per minute** | The pacing ceiling for each sending number. |
| **Quiet hours enabled** | Whether messages are held overnight. |
| **Quiet hours start / end** | First and last **open** hour, in the customer's local time. `8` and `21` means 08:00 up to 21:59. |
| **Release window (minutes)** | Messages held overnight are spread across this window in the morning instead of all firing at once. |
| **Exempt events from quiet hours** | Let event messages (like a dispatch notice) through the quiet window. |
| **Opt-out footer enabled** | Append an opt-out line to SMS. |
| **Opt-out footer text** | The wording, e.g. *"Reply STOP to opt out."* |
| **Failure rate threshold** | The failure rate at which a number is flagged. |
| **Auto-pause unhealthy numbers** | Stop sending from a number that crosses the threshold. |

Leaving a field blank uses the default, and the defaults are shown next to each field — so you do
not have to type a number to find out what it currently does.

<Warning>
  Quiet hours are worked out in the **customer's** local time, using your organization's time zone
  as the base. If your time zone in [Business settings](/settings/business) is wrong, this is wrong
  too.
</Warning>

## "The texts are not going out"

Work down this list:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Is it queued or is it stuck?">
    Check **Waiting** and **Next due at**. If the queue is draining, it is going out — just not yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is it the quiet window?">
    A large **waiting for morning** number means quiet hours are holding it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is a number paused?">
    Look for **needs attention** and check whether auto-pause has kicked in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is it pacing?">
    A low per-number-per-minute setting with a big backlog is slow by design. **Minutes to drain**
    tells you how slow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is the template even active?">
    Back to [Templates](/settings/templates) — an inactive template never sends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Does the sender exist?">
    No default SMS contact means nothing sends at all. See [Profile](/settings/profile).
  </Step>
</Steps>

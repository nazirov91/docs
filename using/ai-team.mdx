**AI Team** is where you set up, teach and test your **Sales assistant**, the AI that drafts or
sends replies to your customers. You teach it by chatting with it in plain words, try it out as if
you were a customer, and decide whether it stays off, writes drafts for your reps, or replies on its
own.

<Note>
  **AI Team** appears in the sidebar, just above **Settings**, only once BeRocker has turned the
  Sales assistant on for your company. Administrators see it first; an administrator can give other
  roles access on the roles screen. Only an admin can change the mode, and only people allowed to
  manage the assistant can teach it or test it. Everyone else sees the page read-only (the box says
  *Only an admin can teach or test it.*).
</Note>

The page has the chat on the left and six tabs on the right: **What it knows**, **How it acts**,
**Handoff**, **Personality**, **Hours** and, after a thin line, **Test**. On a phone the page shows
one thing at a time, and the tab row starts with **Chat** and **Test**, then the five setup tabs;
**Chat** has a dot while a reply is being written or is waiting for you behind another tab. A dot on
**AI Team** in the sidebar means a reply finished while you were on another page.

---

## Off, Suggest or Auto: how the assistant replies

The switch at the top right of the page sets how the assistant handles live customer messages. The
line under **Sales assistant** says what the current mode does.

| Mode | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | *Reps handle every message.* |
| **Suggest** | *It drafts. Your rep sends.* The assistant writes a draft in the Inbox conversation and your rep decides whether to send it. |
| **Auto** | *It replies on its own.* |

Choosing **Auto** asks **Turn on Auto?** first: *It will reply without a rep. Money questions and
complaints still go to a person.* Click **Turn on Auto** to confirm.

A mode that cannot be picked yet shows a lock; hover it to see why (the line under the title also
says). If your deposit, refund or payment answers are switched off while the assistant is on, the
line asks you to switch them back on in **What it knows**.

Other notes at the top of the page:

- *Not responding right now. Reps are handling every message.* The assistant service is down; reps
  get every message. Click **Check again** to retry. While it is down, the chat and most tabs say
  *Not available while the assistant is not responding.* (**Hours** still works.)
- *Replies still come from the older assistant.* The new assistant is not the one replying to
  customers yet.
- *Testing is paused until tomorrow.* The assistant has used its allowance; chat and tests resume
  when it resets. Before that, the box warns when 80% or more is used.

## First visit: getting the assistant ready

The first time you open AI Team, the chat asks a few quick money questions before the assistant can
start drafting (*Let's get … ready. Three quick money questions, then it can start drafting.*).

1. Tap one of the ready answers (the first is marked **Most common**), or type your own in
   **Or type your own answer**.
2. Use **Skip** to leave a question for later, or **Finish later** to stop.
3. At the end it says what it knows, or which deposit, refund or payment answers it still needs. You
   can then choose **Read my website**, **Turn on Suggest** (when allowed), **Not now** or
   **Don't ask again**.

## Teach it: tell it what to know or how to act

The chat is where you teach the assistant. An empty chat asks **What should … know?** and offers
**Read my website**, **Add a file** and **Change an answer**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell it in your own words">
    Type in **Tell it anything, or ask**, for example *We never quote enclosed transport under
    $1,500*. **Enter** sends; **Shift + Enter** starts a new line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the change it proposes">
    It answers with a card showing the tab the change belongs to (such as **What it knows** or
    **How it acts**), what it would save, and, when it changes an answer, what it says now and what
    it would say instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save, edit or cancel">
    Click **Save** (or **Update**), **Edit** to change the wording first, or **Cancel**. A saved
    change turns green with **Saved** and a **Test it** button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Things it did for you show **Undo**. A tab with new items shows a badge such as **2 new**.

### Ask it questions

You can also ask the chat about your assistant: what it told a customer and why, how many
conversations it handled this week, what customers asked that it passed to a person, or what last
night's review found. It reads only your company's data and shows what it looked at. Buttons under
an answer suggest what to ask next.

### Fast or Thorough

The button in the box switches answer speed. **Fast** gives quick answers; **Thorough** thinks
longer before it answers, for harder questions.

### Files and your website

Click **+** in the box:

- **Add a file**: PDF, Word, Markdown, text or zip, up to 10 MB.
- **Read your website**: type your address (for example `yourcompany.com`) and confirm. If you paste
  a lone web address into the box, it offers **Read …?** with **Read it**.

While it reads, a progress bar shows in the box. What it finds waits, switched off, in
**What it knows** until you turn it on.

### Talk instead of typing

Where your browser supports it, the microphone in the box works the same way as in Ask AI: a rising
tone when it starts, a falling tone when it stops, and a sentence under the box if it cannot listen.
The **Test** tab's box has a microphone too; only one of them listens at a time.
See [voice typing](/using/ai-assistant) and [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting).

### Replies in the chat

Under a reply: **Copy**, **Good reply** and **Bad reply** (thumbs down asks **What was wrong?** with
**Wrong**, **Not what I asked**, **Too long** or **Did not do it**), and **Try again** on the last
reply to get a new answer. Use the edit button on your own message to **Edit and send again** (your last
message) or to edit an earlier one, which opens a new chat from that point and leaves this one as it
was. A message you type while a reply is still coming is sent when it finishes (**Cancel** stops
that).

If a reply does not come through, it says why: *The assistant took too long to answer.*, *The
assistant came back with nothing.* or *This reply did not come through.*, with **Try again**.

## Your chats: new chat, rename, pin, delete and Recently deleted

Only you can see your chats.

- **New chat**: the **New chat** button above the chat (**Ctrl + Shift + O**, or **⌘⇧O** on a Mac).
- **Your chats**: the **Your chats** button (a clock) (**Ctrl + Shift + H**, or **⌘⇧H**). Search with **Search chats**;
  chats are grouped **Pinned**, **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days** and **Older**. A flask
  marks a chat that has a test in it. Rename or delete a chat from its row.
- **The chat's name menu** (click the chat name): **Rename**, **Pin** or **Unpin**, **Copy chat**,
  **Save as a file**, **What it remembers** and **Delete**.
- **Delete** keeps the chat under **Recently deleted** for 30 days. Right after deleting, **Undo**
  brings it back; later, open **Recently deleted** at the bottom of your chats and click
  **Bring back**.

## Since you last looked

When you open AI Team, the chat starts with a short note, **Since you last looked**: replies that
failed, how many customer messages it handled and handed to a person, what the overnight review did,
changes you have not tested, and anything still missing before it can go live. Click **Ask** on a
line to dig in, or **Test** on untested changes to open the **Test** tab. Close it with **×**.

---

## What it remembers and the weekly summary

Tell the chat something like *from now on keep answers short* and it remembers it in every chat.

To see the list, click the chat's name and choose **What it remembers**. The window **What it
remembers about you** lists each item with **Forget**. Only you can see them.

The same window has the switch *A summary of last week, every Monday, in a chat of its own*. Turn it
on to get a weekly summary chat each Monday. You can also ask the chat for it.

## Test it as a customer and fix a reply

Testing has its own **Test** tab. Open it from the tab row, with the flask in the chat box (*Test as
a customer. Nothing is sent to anyone.*), or with **Test it** on a saved change, which opens the tab
and starts a test of that change. The chat box always talks to the assistant; the **Test** tab has a
box of its own, where you write as the customer.

The tab's header says **Testing as a customer** and *Nothing is sent to anyone*. While a test runs it
also has **Restart**, which runs the test again from your first line, and **End test**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick something to try, or write as a customer">
    With no test running, the tab says *Pick something to try, or just write as a customer would.*
    and lists things worth trying: a change you have not tested yet (marked with a dot) and common
    customer situations (a tick once tried); **… more to try** shows more. Click one to start, or
    type in the tab's box, **Say something as a customer** (the box says **Nothing is sent**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the reply">
    Your messages are labelled **Customer (you)** and the answers **Sales assistant's reply**. The
    real assistant answers. A reply may say what it used, **Hands this to a rep** when it would pass
    the conversation on, or **Stays silent** with the reason. Where offered, click a reply and then
    **What was sent** to see exactly what the customer would have received. Under the conversation,
    **Try saying** offers what a customer might say next.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix a reply">
    Click **Fix this reply** under a reply. It puts the reply into the chat box, quoted, as
    *In the test, to “…” it answered “…”. It should* (on a new line under anything already in the
    box; on a phone it switches to **Chat**). Finish the sentence with what it should have said and
    send it to the assistant, which saves the change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the test">
    Click **End test**. The tab goes back to what you can try. If you save a change while a test is
    running, the tab says *You saved a change since this test started.* with **Restart with the new
    rules**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With no test running, the **Test** tab also lists the tests you ran in the open chat under
**Earlier tests in this chat**, one card each, **Test: … · … messages**. Click **Open** to read one
again (it is read-only, marked **You were the customer · Ended**), and **Back** to return.

A dot on the flask means you have changes you have not tested yet; opening the **Test** tab clears
it. On a phone the **Test** tab shows a dot too, also when the running test is out of date.

### Rules about tone and wording

You can ask the chat for a warmer tone, different wording, longer or shorter messages, more than one
question in a message, exclamation marks or emoji. It saves the rule together with what the rule
allows, shown on the saved card as an **Allows: …** line.

The saved card also shows the rule tried on one customer message: **Tried on: “…”**, then
**Before: …** and **Now: …**. If the rule did not change that reply, it shows **Now: …** with
*Same answer as before. This rule did not change the reply to that message.* The rule is still
saved.

What a rule can raise:

- up to 3 questions in one message
- up to 480 characters in one message
- up to 3 exclamation marks
- emoji
- phrases you want it to use

Prices, facts, hand-offs to a person and the built-in safety rules cannot be loosened.

<Note>
  Rules about tone saved before this update need to be asked for and saved again.
</Note>

## What it knows

One row per topic, with a count and a **new** badge. Open a row to see each question, its answer and
a switch to turn that answer on or off. Click a line to change it: pick one of the ready answers or
**Something else**, then **Save** (saving switches it on), or **Remove** it.

Answers found in your files, on your website or in what customers asked wait switched off under
their own rows, with where they were found. Use **Turn on all** or **Dismiss all** to handle them
at once.

Keep the deposit, refund and payment answers switched on; the assistant needs them to stay live.

---

## How it acts

Two rows:

- **Guidance**: plain instructions on how it should act, each with a switch. Click one to edit it
  (**Guidance** and **In more words**), **Save**, or **Remove** it. Add new guidance by telling the
  chat.
- **Always**: built-in rules it always follows. They are locked and cannot be changed.

---

## Handoff: when it passes a conversation to a person

Switch each reason on or off. On means it hands the conversation to a rep; off means it keeps helping the customer.

- **Asks for a lower price**
- **Wants a call**
- **Upset or complaining**
- **Asks which carrier**
- **Refund or payment dispute** and **It does not know the answer** are always on and locked.

Then choose:

- **Goes to**: **The lead's rep**, or a specific person.
- **If nobody picks it up**: **Manager after 15 min**, **Manager after 1 hour**, or **Nobody**. If
  nobody answers in time, your administrators and managers get a reminder.

Each change saves at once and shows **Saved.** with **Undo**.

---

## Personality: name and tone

- **Name**: what the assistant calls itself.
- **Tone**: **Friendly**, **Professional** or **Brief**. Each card shows the same sample reply in
  that tone, so you can see the difference.

---

## Hours and people

- **No messages overnight**: the hours it stays quiet, for example **9:00 PM to 7:00 AM**, or
  **Send any time**. This is your company's own quiet-hours setting, not a copy.
- **Works for**: **All reps** or **Chosen reps** (pick them in **Choose reps**).
- **If a rep has not replied**: **Send safe replies after 15 minutes**, **Send safe replies after
  30 minutes** or **Wait for the rep**. Only answers it already knows are sent; the rest stays a
  draft. This works in **Suggest** only.
- **Every night**: **Fix safe things, email me the rest**, **Email me what it found** or **Do
  nothing**. The overnight review reads the day's text conversations; you get one email each morning
  and every fix has **Undo**. Fixes it made itself appear in the chat as **Learned overnight**.

**Marketing** is BeRocker's cold email outreach. You upload a list of people you want to reach
(for example dealerships), write a short series of emails, send them from mailboxes on separate
domains, and work the replies in an inbox and a deal board.

<Note>
  **Marketing** appears in the sidebar only once BeRocker has turned it on for your company, and only
  for roles allowed to open it. Inside, separate permissions decide who can manage campaigns and
  contacts, who can manage mailboxes and domains, who can change Marketing settings, and who can use
  the AI writing helper. Where you lack one, the button is hidden or the screen says *Ask an
  administrator*.
</Note>

The page has seven tabs: **Overview**, **Campaigns**, **Contacts**, **Inbox**, **Pipeline**,
**Senders** and **Settings**.

---

## Get started with Marketing

Until everything is set up, **Overview** shows **Get started with Marketing**: *Three steps and your
first cold email campaign is on its way.*

1. **Choose how you send**: start free from your own inbox, or connect Instantly or Smartlead
   (**Choose** opens **Settings**).
2. **Add contacts**: upload a CSV of the people you want to reach (**Upload** opens **Contacts**).
3. **Create your first campaign**: pick a ready-made sequence or let AI write one (**Create**).

Each step shows **Done** when it is finished.

---

## Marketing overview

**Overview** shows today's numbers: **Sent today**, **Replies**, **Interested**, **Reply rate**,
**Bounce rate**, **Mailbox health** (healthy mailboxes out of all, highlighted when one is not
healthy) and **Open deals** (click it to open the **Pipeline**). Below: a **Sent and replied** chart
per day, and **Needs attention**, a list of anything that needs you (*Nothing needs you right now.*
when all is well). **New campaign** starts a campaign.

---

## Campaigns list

**Campaigns** lists every campaign. *Campaigns send a short series of emails to a contact list and
stop when someone replies.*

- Filter with **All**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Drafts**, **Done** and **Archived**.
- Each campaign shows its status (**Draft**, **Setting up**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Done**,
  **Archived** or **Failed**) and its **Contacts**, **Replied**, **Interested** and **Reply rate**.
- On a row: **Pause** or **Resume**, and under **More actions**: **Open**, **Duplicate** and
  **Archive**. Archiving stops sending for good.

## Create a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New campaign">
    On **Marketing → Campaigns** (or **Overview**), click **New campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick a start">
    Type a **Name**. Under **Start from**, choose **A blank sequence** (write your own emails, or let
    AI write them from a short brief) or one of the ready-made sequences. Click **Create campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through the four steps">
    The campaign builder has four steps: **Audience**, **Sequence**, **Senders & schedule** and
    **Review & launch**. Move with **Back** and **Next**, or click a step. Your work saves as you go
    (**Saving…**, **Saved**, or *Not saved, check your connection*).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only people who manage campaigns can create, edit and launch them.

---

## Campaign builder: Audience

- **Campaign name**: only you and your team see it.
- **Who gets it**: choose one or more contact lists. Upload contacts on the **Contacts** tab first.

---

## Campaign builder: the sequence editor

*Short, plain emails work best: a line about them, a line about you, one question. Each email stops
as soon as the contact replies.*

- Each email has a **Subject** and a **Message**. Click a field chip under the message to insert a
  personal detail, for example `{{first_name}}` or `{{company}}`.
- **Add a follow-up email** adds the next email, up to six. For each follow-up, set **Wait** (days
  after the previous one). Use the arrows to move an email up or down, or remove it.
- **Preview** shows the emails as a contact would see them.
- **Send test to me** emails the sequence to you (it can take a minute to arrive).

### A/B versions

Click **A/B test** on an email to test a second subject or message. Contacts are split between the
versions. You can have up to three versions (A, B and C) of each email. After launch, the campaign's
**Each email** figures mark the **Leading** version.

### The AI helper

**AI** opens the **AI assistant** panel (only for people allowed to use the Marketing AI helper):

- **Write**: fill in **Your company**, **Who you are writing to**, **Your offer**, optional
  **Proof**, the **Tone** (**Friendly**, **Professional**, **Direct** or **Casual**), how many
  **Emails**, and optionally **What should they do?**. Click **Write the sequence**, then **Use
  this** (it replaces every email in the sequence, after you confirm) or **Discard**. Read the draft
  through and make it sound like you.
- **Rewrite**: **Shorter**, **Friendlier**, **More direct**, **Fix grammar** or **Personalize** the
  email you are on.
- **Subjects**: **Suggest 5 subject lines** from the message; click **Use** on one.
- **Spam check**: **Check for spam** gives a **Spam safety** score out of 100 (higher is safer) and
  suggestions, or *No problems found.*

## Campaign builder: Senders & schedule

- **Send from**: **All healthy mailboxes**, or pick mailboxes. Emails rotate between them, so each
  stays under its daily limit.
- **When it sends**: **Days**, **From** and **Until**, and **Time zone**. Emails are spread across
  the window. **Count business days only** makes "wait 3 days" skip weekends.
- **Pace and behaviour**: **Emails per mailbox per day** (the screen warns above 50, and suggests
  new mailboxes start around 10 a day and add 5 a week), **Stop when they reply** (leave this on),
  and **Track opens** (adds an invisible image; it makes more email land in spam, so replies are the
  number to watch).

On the free Lite engine your whole account has a daily sending cap, shown on this step.

---

## Campaign builder: Review & launch and the launch checklist

**Review & launch** sums up the campaign (audience, emails, senders and schedule; **Edit** jumps
back) and shows **Ready to launch?**, a checklist that must be all done:

- **Audience**
- **Every email has a subject and a body**
- **Unsubscribe link**
- **Postal address in the footer**
- **At least one mailbox**
- **Mailboxes warmed up**
- **Domain records**
- **Sending allowance**
- **Sending engine connected**
- **Sending switched on**

**Send the whole sequence to me** emails you every email. When the checklist is complete, click
**Launch campaign**. Otherwise it says *Fix the items above to launch.*

---

## A running campaign: pause, edit, duplicate, archive

Open a campaign to see its status and **Contacts**, **Sent**, **Replied**, **Interested**,
**Bounced** and **Unsubscribed**, the figures for **Each email**, and the contacts in it.

- **Pause** stops sending: *Nobody gets an email until you resume it.* **Resume** starts again.
- **Edit sequence** works only while the campaign is paused. Click **Done** when finished, then
  resume.
- **Duplicate** copies it as a new draft. **Archive** stops it for good.
- **Open Inbox** jumps to its replies.
- In the contacts list, filter by status (**Queued**, **In sequence**, **Replied**, **Bounced**,
  **Unsubscribed**, **Finished**, **Removed**) and **Remove** a contact so they get no more emails
  from this campaign.

If a campaign stopped because of a problem, it says why. Check **Senders** and **Settings**, then
resume it.

## Contacts, lists and Do not email

**Contacts** has two views: **Lists** and **Do not email**.

### Upload contacts from a CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Upload CSV">
    Under **Lists**, click **Upload CSV**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the list and file">
    Choose **Add to list** (an existing list, or name a new one) and **Choose a CSV file**: up to
    10 MB, one row per person, with an email column. Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the columns">
    It shows how many rows it found and the columns it matched. Fix anything that is off, then click
    **Import … rows**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    It says how many contacts were added and how many were left out to protect your sending
    reputation. A large file keeps importing if you close the window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Work with lists and contacts

Pick **All contacts** or a list. Search by name, email or company, and filter by **Email check**
(**Not checked**, **Valid**, **Risky**, **Invalid**). **Add more** uploads into the list you are on.
You can delete a contact (they are removed from the list and from campaigns not sent yet) or a whole
list.

### Do not email

*Nobody here gets a Marketing email, from any campaign.* The list shows why each address is there
(**Unsubscribed**, **Bounced**, **Spam complaint**, **Added by hand** or **Customer**).
**Add addresses** adds emails (one per line, or separated by commas) with an optional note.
**Remove** lets an address be emailed again; bounced and complained addresses stay blocked to
protect your mailboxes.

## Marketing inbox: replies to your campaigns

**Inbox** collects replies, sorted by kind. Filter with **Unhandled**, **Interested** or **All**,
and click a reply to read it.

- Each reply has a kind: **Interested**, **Not now**, **Not interested**, **Out of office**,
  **Wrong person**, **Auto-reply**, **Unsubscribe**, **Bounce**, **Other** or **Not sorted**. Change
  it if it is wrong.
- **Add to pipeline** puts the contact on the **Pipeline** board (or **View on board** if they are
  already there).
- **Create lead** makes a lead in the CRM, or **Open lead** if one exists.
- **Reply** sends your answer from the mailbox that sent the campaign.
- **Mark handled** clears it from **Unhandled**.

---

## Pipeline: the Marketing deal board

**Pipeline** is a board of deals from your campaigns, in stages. The tab shows how many cards are
open.

- Filter with **All** or **Mine**, by campaign, or search by name, company or campaign.
- Drag a card to another stage, or use **Move**. Moving a card to a **Won** stage asks once
  (**Mark as won**) and can create a lead in the CRM for them. Moving to a **Lost** stage asks why
  (optional) with **Mark as lost**.
- **Add stage** adds a stage (up to 15) with a **Name**, **Colour** and **Type**: **Open** (still in
  progress), **Won** or **Lost**. A stage's menu offers **Rename, colour and type**, **Move left**,
  **Move right** and **Delete stage** (you choose where its cards go).

Click a card to open it: set the **Owner**, **Deal value** and **Next step** with a date (it warns
when overdue), **Open lead**, **Create lead** or **Open in Inbox**, read and reply to the
**Conversation**, add **Notes and activity**, or **Remove from board** (their replies stay in the
Inbox).

## Senders: mailboxes and domains

Send cold email from separate domains so your main domain's reputation stays safe. **Senders** has
two views: **Mailboxes** and **Domains**.

### Add a mailbox

Click **Add mailbox**.

- On the free **Lite** engine you pick an inbox already connected to BeRocker, so emails appear in
  your Sent folder.
- Otherwise click **Connect Google Workspace**, or enter SMTP and IMAP details for **Google
  Workspace**, **Microsoft 365** or **Other**: **Email**, **Sender name**, servers and ports,
  **Username** and password (for Google Workspace, an **App password**). Click **Connect mailbox**.
  Warm-up starts on its own.

Each mailbox card shows its health, status (**Sending**, **Paused**, **Disconnected** or **Needs
attention**), **Sent today** out of its daily limit (click the limit to change it) and **Warm-up**.
Its menu has **Pause this mailbox**, **Resume this mailbox** and **Remove**. Lite has no warm-up.
**Sync** brings in mailboxes from Instantly or Smartlead.

### Add a sending domain

- **Buy a new domain**: search for a name that looks like yours (never your main domain), choose
  where its mailboxes will be, and confirm. The email records are written for you. Purchases are
  non-refundable and renew yearly. This needs Vercel connected in **Settings**.
- **Add a domain I own**: type the domain and click **Check**, then choose how its records are set:
  automatically through Cloudflare or Porkbun, by pointing its nameservers to BeRocker, or by adding
  them yourself with a copy button and help for your DNS host. You can also set a **Tracking
  domain**, needed only when open or click tracking is on.

Each domain shows **Waiting for records**, **Checking**, **Ready** or **Needs attention**, and its
**MX**, **SPF**, **DKIM**, **DMARC** and **Tracking** records. Use **Re-check** (or **Check now**),
**Apply again** to rewrite automatic records, **Edit**, and **Remove from BeRocker** (its DNS
records stay as they are). If a record with the same host already exists, such as SPF, edit it
rather than adding a second one.

## Marketing settings

**Settings** (only people who manage Marketing settings can change it; others see it read-only):

- **How you send**: one engine at a time. **Lite** is free and sends a limited number of emails a
  day from your own inbox; **Instantly** and **Smartlead** give unlimited mailboxes with warm-up on
  your own account. **Connect** or **Manage** each. Switching engine asks first; mailboxes are per
  engine, so you may need to add them again. Pause all campaigns before disconnecting the engine
  they send with.
- **Sending window**: the default **Days**, **From**, **Until** and **Time zone** for new campaigns.
- **Emails per mailbox per day**.
- **Footer**: **Postal address** (required by law in every commercial email; campaigns cannot
  launch without it) and **Unsubscribe wording** (the link itself is always added).
- **What happens with replies**: **Create leads from replies** (interested and "not now" replies
  become leads, assigned like any new lead), **Stop on reply**, **Notify me about interested
  replies** (the campaign owner gets a notification) and **Track opens**.
- **Domains**: connect Vercel, Cloudflare or Porkbun with an API key or token. **Save & test**
  checks the connection.

Click **Save settings** when done.

**Carriers** is the record of every carrier you have worked with. Carriers arrive here
automatically the first time you dispatch a load to them from a load board, and you can add one
by hand at any time.

## The list

The stats strip at the top counts your book: **Total carriers**, **Verified**, **New this month**,
**Carriers with loads**, **Dispatched loads** — plus your **Busiest carriers** by load count.

The table itself shows each carrier's **Name**, **Contact**, **Phone**, **Email**, **MC**, **US
DOT**, **Status**, **Terms**, **Loads**, **Verified**, **Rating**, **Board** (the load board it came
from), **Address** and **Added**; **Manage columns** chooses which are shown. Search by name, MC,
DOT or email, and **Filters** narrows by **Status**, **Verified**, **Has loads** and **Load board**.
**Add Carrier** creates one by hand.

---

## Verification

A carrier can carry a **verification** result from a LongMile check: a status, a score, a band and
a recommendation, with the date it was checked.

That check looks at federal records — authority, insurance, safety history, bond, and
chameleon-carrier detection — and gives you a 100-point score so you are not deciding on gut feel.
See [LongMile](/integrations/loadboards/longmile).

<Info>
  A carrier that has never been checked shows no verification, which is different from failing one.
</Info>

## The carrier profile

Open a carrier and you get five tabs — **Loads**, **Profile**, **Documents**, **Due Diligence**
and **Notes** — above a performance strip (**Loads hauled**, **Carrier pay**, **Paid to carrier**,
**Offer acceptance**, **Last load**):

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Loads">
    Every load this carrier has hauled for you, with the rate paid and the outcome. Opens on this
    tab, because it is the question you usually have.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Profile">
    Company name, contact name, phone, email, address, website, company type, MC number and US DOT
    number, DOT certificate and insurance certificate, load board and load board ID, rating, plus
    their default **payment terms**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Documents">
    The general document shelf — signed carrier agreements, W-9s, anything you keep on file.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Due Diligence">
    The **Carrier verification** panel — run a LongMile check from here with **Verify** — plus
    compliance paperwork (**Upload due diligence report**) kept separately from general documents,
    and counted separately, so an audit trail stays an audit trail.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Notes">
    Free-text notes from your team. Use it for the things that never fit a field — who to ask for,
    which lanes they actually run, why you stopped using them.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

---

## Payment terms

Each carrier has a **terms** value in days — how long after delivery you pay them. It is set on the
Profile tab and is used as the default when you post a load with that carrier.

Amounts owed and paid are tracked in [Accounting](/using/accounting).

**Follow up** is where you see who to call or answer next. It has two views, switched with
**List** and **Calendar** at the top of the page:

- **List** is the work list: everything waiting on you in one list, with the lead open beside it.
- **Calendar** shows follow-up calls, calling-schedule calls and tasks by time.

You open it from **Follow up** in the sidebar. Your role needs access to scheduled calls to see it.

---

## The Follow up work list

The left side of the **List** view is the work list. At the top:

- A big number with **things need you** (or **thing needs you**), and a refresh button.
- Three tiles: **waiting** (customers waiting on a reply, missed calls included), **due today**
  (calls you scheduled for today, and calling-schedule calls that are due) and **done today**
  (calls, texts and outcomes logged today). Click **waiting** or **due today** to jump to that part
  of the list.
- A progress bar, *X done, Y left today*.
- A search box, **Name or lead #**.
- Three filters: **All**, **Reply** and **Calls**, each with a count.

The list is grouped. Click a group heading to fold it or open it.

| Group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs driver ASAP** | Leads you pinned with the **Driver ASAP** outcome. Always on top. |
| **Reply now** | Customers whose last message is newer than yours, newest first. Missed calls with no call back sit under them, counted as **Call back**. |
| **Scheduled by me** | Follow-up calls you set: today by time, then the coming days. |
| **Calls due** | The calling schedule's calls that are due now, oldest first. Log what happened and the schedule moves on. |
| **Not set** | Your new leads with nothing else on them, nearest pickup date first, grouped by pickup date (for example **Pickup in the next 7 days**, **No pickup date**). |

Each row shows the customer's name, the lead number, a chip for its group, a **Rescue** or **Ready
to close** chip when the AI flagged the conversation, and a short reason such as *texted 2h ago*
or *missed call 3:10 PM*. When a group has more rows, **Show N more** loads them.

When new work arrives while you are working, a **N new · Refresh** button appears instead of the
list jumping. The list also refreshes on its own about once a minute while the tab is open.

When everything is handled you see **All done**, with the time of your next call and **Open the
calendar**.

## Work a lead from the Follow up list

Click a row to open it. The lead opens on the right with its conversation and its details (the
same **Details**, **Booking** and **Carrier** panel as the Inbox). On narrow screens a **Show**
switch flips between **Conversation** and **Details**.

The open row shows a strip of actions:

1. **Reply** (or press R) puts you in the conversation on the right. On a call row this button is
   the call result instead: pick **Talked**, **No answer** or **Left message**.
2. **Call** calls the customer, when calling is set up and the lead has a phone number. If the
   phone system logged the call, the row asks **How did it go?** with the three call results.
3. **Follow up** (the calendar button, or press N) sets when to follow up next: **In 2 hours**,
   **Tomorrow 9 AM**, **In 3 days**, **Next Monday 9 AM**, **Pick a date…**, or **No date** to
   clear it.
4. **Done, next** (or Enter) saves what you picked and opens the next lead.

The **More** menu (three dots) holds the rest:

- **Log a call**: **Talked**, **No answer**, **Left message** (on a call row, **Text instead**).
- **Mark as**: **Wrong number** (archives the lead), **Price up on CD**, **Driver ASAP** (pins the
  lead on top until it books) and **Not interested** (archives the lead).
- **Add a note**: a line for the lead's timeline.
- **Tomorrow** (or T): nothing to log, takes the lead off today's list and brings it back
  tomorrow.
- **Unpin**, on a pinned lead.
- **Mark dead**: archives the lead. Shown only to people allowed to archive, and only on leads
  still in sales.

After every action a bar at the bottom offers **Undo** for ten seconds (or press U).

## Follow up keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** or click the keyboard button on the **List** view to see them.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| J or ↓ | Next lead |
| K or ↑ | Previous lead |
| 1 / 2 / 3 | Talked / No answer / Left message |
| N | Pick when to follow up |
| Enter | Done, next lead |
| D | Done, stay on this lead |
| T | Tomorrow |
| R | Reply in the conversation |
| U | Undo the last action |
| / | Search |
| Esc | Close the lead, back to the list |
| ? | Show these shortcuts |

## See a teammate's Follow up list

If your role can see other people's follow-ups, a row of people appears at the top of the **List**
view: **Everyone**, then **Me**, then each teammate with a count of what is waiting and due on
them. Pick a person to see their list, or **Everyone** for the whole team's. With **Everyone**
picked, each row shows who owns it.

---

## Your day: the Today button

**Today** at the top of the **List** view opens a side panel of today's calls and tasks in time
order, with a red **Now** line. Click an item with a lead to open that lead. With **Everyone**
picked, the panel still shows your own day (**Your day**).

---

## The Follow up calendar

Click **Calendar** at the top of Follow up. It shows follow-up calls, the calling schedule's calls
and tasks by time. It is the CRM's own calendar and does not connect to Google or Outlook.

- **Today**, the arrows, and **Day**, **Week**, **Month** or **Agenda** move around. The view you
  pick is remembered.
- The legend says what the colours mean: **Follow-up call**, **Calling schedule** and **Task**.
- If calls are overdue, a red chip shows *N overdue* (and how many have no time). Click it to go
  back to the list.
- Times are your local time. Hover a call to see the customer's time.
- If your role can see other people's follow-ups, the **Just me** box lets you add colleagues'
  calendars. Each person then gets their own colour.

Click an item to see **When**, **Who**, **Source** and **Note**, with these buttons:

- **Open lead** opens the lead.
- **Reschedule** opens the **Schedule follow-up call** window (calls only).
- **Back to calling schedule** removes a follow-up call you set, so the lead follows the calling
  schedule again.

If your role can schedule follow-up calls, you can also drag a call to another time, or select it
and use Alt plus the arrow keys.

Calendar keys: **T** for today, **N** and **P** (or J and K) for the next or previous period, and
**D**, **W**, **M**, **A** for day, week, month or agenda.

## Schedule a follow-up call

A follow-up call is a call for your team to make at a set time. It does not change your automated
follow-up texts and emails, which are a separate schedule.

You can schedule one from the calendar button in the conversation header in the Inbox (its tooltip
reads **Schedule a follow-up call**, or **Next call:** with the time once one is set), from the
Dispatch quick view, and from **Reschedule** on the calendar. Your role needs permission to
schedule follow-up calls.

1. Open **Schedule follow-up call**. If the lead already has a call, the window shows it (**Set
   for…** or **Calling schedule: next call…**).
2. Pick a **Quick pick**: **In 1 hour**, **In 3 hours**, **Tomorrow 9 AM**, **In 2 days** or **In 1
   week**. Each shows the exact time it sets, and the one you used last comes first. Or pick a
   **Date and time** yourself. Past days cannot be picked.
3. Check the line under the date. It shows what time it will be for the customer, warns when that
   is *outside 8 AM to 9 PM for them*, and warns when you already have several calls around that
   hour.
4. Add a **Comment** if you like. It shows on the lead timeline.
5. Click **Save**. You see **Follow-up call scheduled**.

To remove a call you set and let the lead follow the calling schedule again, click **Back to
calling schedule** in the same window.

## Follow-up calls vs follow-up messages

| | [Follow-up messages](/settings/templates) | Follow up work list |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What** | Timed texts and emails that go out on their own | A list of people a human should answer or call |
| **When** | Fixed intervals after a quote | As customers reply, calls are missed and calls come due |
| **Best at** | Chasing people who never replied | Answering and closing people who did |

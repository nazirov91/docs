**Dispatch** is the operations view: one map of everything currently moving, with four queues around
it. It refreshes itself, shows **Updated** *x ago* in the map header, and the refresh button at
the top reloads it on demand. A stat bar above the map counts **In transit**, **Overdue pickup**,
**Upcoming** and **SD Requests**.

## The map

Each pin is a load. Click one to see the lane, **Pickup**, **Delivery**, **Price** and **Carrier**,
and **View load** to jump into the conversation.

Positions come from two places, and the popup tells you which:

| Note on the popup | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Live GPS · updated** *x ago* | The driver's real position, reported by Super Dispatch, with the place name where known. |
| **Estimated position from pickup & delivery dates — not live GPS** | Worked out from the dates. |

The **Live GPS** switch in the map header shows only the loads with a real GPS position. Turn it
off to see estimated positions for every load. **Re-center** puts the map back over your loads
after you have panned away.

<Warning>
  If you see **"Mapbox access token not configured"**, the app has no Mapbox key and cannot draw
  the map. The dispatch queues below still work without it. Contact
  [BeRocker support](/support/contact).
</Warning>

## The queues

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="On the road" icon="truck">
    Every load currently in transit. Empty until carriers start picking up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Overdue pickups" icon="circle-exclamation">
    Loads whose pickup date has passed with no pickup reported. Work these first.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Coming up soon" icon="calendar">
    Pickups scheduled in the next two weeks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SD Requests" icon="inbox">
    Posted loads with open Super Dispatch offers waiting on a yes or no.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Clicking a load in **SD Requests** opens its conversation straight on the **Carrier** tab, because
that is where you accept or decline the offer. Clicking a load in any other queue opens the
conversation on the default **Details** tab.

---

## Filters

The filter bar at the top is the same one the Dashboard uses: a **date range** and a **Teams /
Users** picker (**Search team…**). Narrow it to one agent to see just their loads.

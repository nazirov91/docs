**Dispatch** is where you move paid loads onto trucks and keep them moving. The screen has a small
**Dispatch Map** at the top and five tabs underneath, one for each job a dispatcher does. You open
it from **Dispatch** in the sidebar. Anyone who can see the Shipments list can open it.

---

## The Dispatch tabs

Each tab shows a count. The count turns red when at least one load in the tab is late or needs a
decision now. A line under the tabs says what the list is.

| Tab | What is in it | The date column |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Offers** | Loads where carriers are waiting on your answer on Super Dispatch. Loads with the most offers come first. | **Offers**, for example *3 offers · 1 counter sent · 1 awaiting carrier* |
| **To post** | Paid loads that are not on a load board yet. Canceled loads picking up soon come first, so you can decide whether to re-post them. | **Pickup** |
| **Pickups** | Loads waiting on a truck. Overdue pickups first, then the soonest pickup in the next two weeks. | **Pickup**, for example *3 days overdue*, *Today*, *Tomorrow*, *In 4 days* |
| **On the road** | Loads with a carrier, dispatched or picked up. These are the trucks on the map. Soonest delivery first. | **Delivery**, for example *2 days late* |
| **Call the driver** | Loads whose pickup or delivery date passed and nobody marked the load. | **Was due**, for example *Delivery, 3 days ago* |

Each row shows **Load**, **Customer**, **Route**, **Vehicle** (or **Carrier** on **On the road**
and **Call the driver**), the date column, **Status** and **Price**, plus a chat button.

A tab shows its first 10 loads. Click **Show all** to see the rest of what was loaded. When a tab
holds more loads than the page carries, it says *Showing X of Y. The rest are on the Shipments
page.*

When a tab is empty it says so, for example **No carrier offers waiting on you right now.** or
**No pickups in the next two weeks.**

---

## Open a load from Dispatch

- **Click a row** to open the load quick view (see below) without leaving the page.
- **Click the load number**, or Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on a Mac) or middle-click a row, to open the
  load in a new browser tab. Loads from the **Offers** tab open on their **Carrier** tab, where the
  offers are.
- **Click the chat button** at the end of the row to open the load's conversation.

---

## The load quick view

Clicking a row on any Dispatch tab opens a quick view of that load. Across the top it shows the
load number, its status, the tab it came from and the customer's name, then one line that says
what the load needs, in the words of that tab. For example: *Paid but not on a load board, and the
pickup is 2 days overdue. Post it now.*

The quick view has two tabs:

- **Overview**: **Pickup** and **Delivery** places and dates, the distance, where the driver was
  last seen (when known), the **Carrier** card (name, contact, phone, email, MC number and agreed
  price), the **Customer** card (phone and email, with a copy button on each), **Money**
  (**Price**, **Broker fee**, **Carrier pay**), the **Vehicle** list with the trailer type, and
  **Notes**.
- **Conversation**: the customer conversation, read in place.

Use the arrows in the header, or the Left and Right arrow keys, to step to the previous or next
load in the same tab. Press Esc or **Close** to close it.

The buttons at the bottom:

- **Open full view** opens the whole load.
- **Review offers** (loads from the **Offers** tab) opens the load straight on its carrier offers.
  **Open full view** is next to it.
- **Schedule a follow-up call** opens the **Schedule follow-up call** window for this load. Shown
  only if your role can schedule follow-up calls.

If the load moves to another tab while the quick view is open, a note says *This load has moved
out of [tab] since you opened it. It may have been updated.*

## The Dispatch Map

The **Dispatch Map** at the top shows the trucks on the **On the road** tab. Its header shows
**LIVE**, the number of loads on the road, and **Updated** with the time the board was last read.

- Click a truck to see its route, pickup, delivery, price and carrier, and to open the load.
- **Live GPS** shows only the trucks sharing a live driver location through Super Dispatch. With
  it off, positions are estimated from the pickup and delivery dates.
- **Re-center** puts the map back over your loads.
- **Hide map** folds the map away and **Show map** brings it back. Your choice is remembered in
  this browser. The tabs and the **Updated** time keep working with the map hidden.

When nothing can be drawn, the map says why, for example **No live GPS positions yet** (turn off
**Live GPS** to see estimated positions) or **No loads on the road right now**.

<Warning>
  If the map shows *The map needs a Mapbox key, which isn't set up yet. The tabs below work without
  it.*, the map cannot be drawn on your account. Everything else on the page still works. Contact
  [BeRocker support](/support/contact).
</Warning>

On a phone, the tabs and the list come first and the map comes after them.

---

## Filter and refresh Dispatch

The filter bar at the top is the same one the Dashboard uses: a date range and a team or user
picker (**Search team...**). Pick one agent to see only their loads, or a team to see that team's
loads.

The board refreshes on its own while the page is open and pauses when you leave it. The refresh
button at the top right reloads it now. If a refresh fails, a red bar says *Couldn't refresh the
board. Showing what it looked like at [time].* with **Try again**.

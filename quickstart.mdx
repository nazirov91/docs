title: "Quickstart"
description: "Get your BeRocker account from empty to taking live leads in about 30 minutes."
---

Work through these seven steps in order. Each one links to the full page if you want the detail.

<Info>
  Steps 1–3 are for **every user**. Steps 4–7 need an admin or manager role.
</Info>

---

## 1. Sign in

Go to [app.berocker.com](https://app.berocker.com) and sign in with the email and password your
admin gave you.

If your company requires two-factor authentication you will be asked for a code the first time.
See [Team and security](/settings/team-and-security).

---

## 2. Set up your profile

**Settings → Profile.**

Add the phone number and email address you will use to talk to customers, and mark one of each as
default. If you use RingCentral, click **Register** next to the number to authorise it.

<Warning>
  Until you add a default SMS/call contact you cannot text or call customers from a chat.
</Warning>

Full page: [Profile](/settings/profile)

---

## 3. Learn the layout

Five minutes here saves hours later — [Tour the CRM](/get-started/tour).

The short version: **Inbox** is where you talk to customers, **Shipments** is the table of every
lead, **Follow up** tells you who needs you today, and **Dispatch** is the live map of loads on the
road.

---

## 4. Fill in your company details

**Settings → Business.**

Account name, address, time zone, MC and DOT numbers, logo and brand colours. These appear on every
quote, contract, invoice and email you send.

Full page: [Business](/settings/business)

---

## 5. Connect your tools

**Settings → Integrations.**

Connect at least one from each row you need:

| What for | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Calls and SMS | Twilio, RingCentral, Telnyx, Vonage, Quo, Zoom Phone |
| Email | SendGrid, Mailgun, Gmail |
| Load boards | Central Dispatch, Super Dispatch, LongMile |
| Payments | Stripe, Square, Authorize.Net, RevitPay, QuickBooks |

Full page: [Integrations](/settings/integrations)

---

## 6. Point your leads at BeRocker

**Settings → Lead Management → Lead sources.**

Create one lead source per website or provider, then copy its endpoint URL and give it to that
provider — or POST your website form to it.

Full pages: [Lead sources](/settings/lead-sources) · [Lead source API](/integrations/lead-source-api)

---

## 7. Add your team and split the leads

**Settings → Team and security → People** to invite agents, then
**Settings → Lead Management → Lead distribution** to set how many leads each agent gets per day.

Full pages: [Team and security](/settings/team-and-security) · [Lead distribution](/settings/lead-distribution)
